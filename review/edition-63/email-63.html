Inter play at Feyenoord tonight at 3:00 PM ET with Alessandro Bastoni, the Italy defender, suspended. Cristian Chivu, Inter’s head coach, said he still believes in this squad, and that the defender Benjamin Pavard and the goalkeeper Ivan Provedel are available again. Both teams have three points from three Champions League matches.

CONFIRMED · CLUB · LEAGUE · UEFA

Bastoni is suspended, and Nyberg has the whistle

Bastoni is the only Inter player suspended tonight. The ban is a European one and does not touch Serie A.

Glenn Nyberg, the Swedish referee, takes the match. Kickoff at De Kuip, Feyenoord’s stadium in Rotterdam, is 3:00 PM ET — 21:00 CET. Feyenoord are unbeaten and first in the Dutch league.

INTER.IT · NOV 3

Chivu and Thuram speak in Rotterdam

Chivu said “Io credo nel lavoro, credo in questa squadra” — I believe in the work, I believe in this squad. By his reckoning about 16 points make the top eight. Inter have three. He confirmed that Pavard and Provedel are back in the squad.

Marcus Thuram, the France forward, was asked about Nicolò Barella and Bastoni. Both said after the derby that the team has problems to fix. “Condivido, loro sono due leader” — I agree, they are two leaders. A problem has to be named and corrected, not ignored, he said.

SAID · INTER.IT · NOV 2

A double-header in Rotterdam, and the women’s week

Inter’s Under 19 side play Feyenoord in the UEFA Youth League at 8:00 AM ET — 14:00 CET — at the Varkenoord complex beside De Kuip, streamed on Inter TV.

Inter Women play at AC Milan on Saturday, November 7, the season’s first women’s derby. Manchester City visit in the Women’s Champions League on Tuesday, November 10 at 3:00 PM ET — 21:00 CET.

REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

The eleven the papers expect at De Kuip

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy’s biggest sports daily, expects Chivu to keep Djed Spence, the England full back, at right back. Yann Bisseck and Carlos Augusto go beside him. Carlos Augusto drops back to cover for Bastoni. Federico Dimarco returns on the left. Hakan Calhanoglu plays deep, Barella and Piotr Zielinski ahead of him, Francesco Pio Esposito next to Thuram.

Tuttosport sets out a 3–5–2 with Manuel Akanji in the back three. It lists Luis Henrique, the Brazilian winger, among the injured, a day after it expected him to start. Inter have published no lineup.

UPDATE · GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · NOV 3

Corriere dello Sport on the three English signings

Corriere dello Sport says Inter’s three signings from the Premier League have given Chivu almost nothing because of injuries. Curtis Jones came from Liverpool at the coach’s request. The midfielder stopped again in the October international break. John Stones, signed on a free transfer, has continued a run of unavailability going back several seasons. Spence is not yet at full fitness.
