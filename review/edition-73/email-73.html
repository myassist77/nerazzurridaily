Italy beat France 3–1 at San Siro on Thursday. The Inter forward Francesco Pio Esposito scored twice — his first double for Italy. The Italy midfielder Davide Frattesi scored the other, turning in a cross from the Inter defender Federico Dimarco. Italy and France now lead Nations League Group 1 with 10 points each. Italy need a draw in Brussels on Sunday to reach the quarterfinals.

Pio Esposito’s double turns Italy around

France led inside 10 minutes. The forward Michael Olise scored after a mistake by the goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma. Pio Esposito equalized in the 26th minute.

Frattesi put Italy ahead in the 63rd minute, shortly after Dimarco came on. Pio Esposito made it 3–1 from the penalty spot in the 75th. The Inter defender Alessandro Bastoni played all 90 minutes, and the midfielder Nicolò Barella started. The Italian federation counted a crowd of 71,452.

Sucic scores at Wembley, and Turkey lose again

The Inter midfielder Petar Sucic started for Croatia at Wembley. He equalized in the 59th minute, but England won 2–1. He came off in the 70th. Croatia are out of the quarterfinals.

Hakan Çalhanoğlu, another Inter midfielder, came on in the 58th minute in Izmir. Belgium beat Turkey 2–1, and the Belgium goalkeeper saved his late shot. Turkey are last in Italy’s group with no points.

INTER.IT · NOV 12

The run to Christmas ends with the Supercoppa against Lazio

Inter published their schedule through Christmas on Friday. It lists nine matches. The first is away to Atalanta on Sunday, November 22. The last is the Supercoppa Italiana — Italy’s super cup — against Lazio at San Siro. That is on Tuesday, December 22, at 3:00 PM ET, 21:00 CET.

The club does not say which round the Supercoppa tie is, and has set no ticket date.

Carlos Augusto’s renewal is now said to be close

Yesterday’s edition reported that pay was holding up the defender Carlos Augusto’s new contract. Sky Sport Italia now puts the deal in its closing stages. The extension would run to 2031, and news could come within days.

FCInterNews, which carried the report, says there is “ottimismo da entrambe le parti” — optimism on both sides — and prints no salary figure. Inter have announced nothing.

UPDATE · SKY SPORT ITALIA VIA FCINTERNEWS · NOV 12

Bastoni is reported clear for Sunday after a scoreboard error

The screen at San Siro showed Bastoni’s name for a booking on Thursday. That would have kept him out of Belgium–Italy on Sunday. Sky Sport Italia’s Peppe Di Stefano reported from the stadium that the screen was wrong. The player booked was Frattesi.

Di Stefano said an Italy delegate confirmed to him that “l’ammonito è Davide Frattesi” — the booked player is Frattesi. UEFA had made the error, he said. The federation’s match report lists Frattesi among the cautions, not Bastoni.
