Atalanta wait for Inter in Bergamo

Inter visit Atalanta at the Gewiss Stadium on Sunday at 2:45 PM ET, 20:45 CET, in Serie A matchday 12. Inter are second with 28 points, one behind Lazio. Atalanta are 12th with 14.

Their coach, Maurizio Sarri, came from Lazio. Atalanta won at Monza last time out, after four league games without a win. The striker Nikola Krstović leads them with seven goals, and the wing-back Nicola Zalewski is a former Inter player. Inter’s own preview says they have conceded when opponents overload a flank and cross into the box.

Inter U23 host the Serie C leaders

Inter’s Under-23 side, coached by Stefano Vecchi, play Potenza on Saturday at 8:30 AM ET, 14:30 CET, at the Stadio Breda in Sesto San Giovanni, just north of Milan. Inter are eighth in their Serie C group with 20 points and have a game in hand, away to Audace Cerignola on Wednesday.

Potenza lead with 35 points from 14 games. They have the best attack in the group, with 39 goals, and have won their last three.

INTER.IT · NOV 19

Thuram wins Goal of the Month

Inter fans voted the forward Marcus Thuram’s goal against Fiorentina on October 25 the club’s goal of the month for October. He cut in from the right in stoppage time and hit a low left-footed shot into the corner. It tied the game, and Inter went on to win 2–1.

Lautaro set for the bench in Bergamo

Corriere dello Sport says Thuram and the striker Francesco Pio Esposito will start, with Lautaro Martínez on the bench as he builds up his fitness. The midfielder Piotr Zieliński has a sore foot, which the paper calls nothing serious, and the wing-back Carlos Augusto is back from international duty.

La Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy’s biggest sports daily, says only the striker Ange-Yoan Bonny and the midfielder Aleksandar Stanković are out. It adds that the defender Manuel Akanji is carrying a knock, which could move the defender Yann Bisseck into the middle and bring the defender Benjamin Pavard back.
