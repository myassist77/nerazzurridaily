Inter won 3–0 at Feyenoord on Tuesday night, an answer to the derby defeat by AC Milan at San Siro. The defender Yann Bisseck, the forward Francesco Pio Esposito and the midfielder Piotr Zieliński scored at De Kuip. Cristian Chivu, Inter’s head coach, said the performance gave the squad confidence. The win lifts Inter to 14th of the 36 teams in the league phase.

The win moves Inter up to 14th in the league phase

Inter now have six points from four matches, and the club’s table places them 14th of 36. Paris Saint-Germain lead the table with 12 points. Barcelona and Aston Villa have nine.

Four matches are left: VfB Stuttgart and Liverpool at home, Borussia Dortmund and Slovan Bratislava away. Each kicks off at 3:00 PM ET, 21:00 CET.

INTER.IT · NOV 3

Chivu and his three scorers speak in Rotterdam

Cristian Chivu said Inter had talked through what was going wrong and then put the answers on the field. “This performance gives us a lot of confidence and something to build on,” he said. Consistency, he added, was what the team was still looking for.

Bisseck, who opened the scoring, said his celebration “just came to me,” and that he had not really thought about it. Esposito said great sides reply right away. Zieliński said the whole team defended well and gave Feyenoord no space.

SAID · INTER.IT · NOV 3

A Youth League loss, and a derby for the women

Inter’s under-19 side lost 3–1 at Feyenoord earlier the same day. Their goal came in the 20th minute, and they have four points from four Youth League matches.

The women’s team beat Lazio 3–0 on Sunday, with two goals from Haley Bugeja, the Inter forward. They play the season’s first Milan derby on Saturday. No kickoff time has been published. On Tuesday they host Manchester City in the Women’s Champions League at 3:00 PM ET, 21:00 CET, in Monza.

Sky Sport Italia says Dimarco is tied to 2028

Sky Sport Italia reports that Inter have used a clause in Federico Dimarco’s old deal. It carries the contract of their left wing-back to June 30, 2028. The report says Dimarco would prefer a longer agreement, to 2029 or 2030. Inter have announced nothing.

SKY SPORT ITALIA VIA FCINTERNEWS · NOV 3

The marks the Italian press gave at De Kuip

FCInterNews gave sevens out of ten to Bisseck, Zieliński, Chivu and Carlos Augusto, the Inter defender. Djed Spence, the Inter wing-back, took six and a half for a hand in the first two goals. Esposito took the same mark and was, the site wrote, a few centimeters short of a second goal.
