Twentieth of 36, and Rotterdam next

Inter are 20th of 36 in the league phase, level with Manchester United. They lost 2–1 at Real Madrid, lost 4–3 at home to Club Brugge, then beat Shakhtar Donetsk 1–0 through a goal from the defender Carlos Augusto. The top 24 go through.

Chivu’s press conference is at De Kuip at 12:45 PM ET today — 18:45 CET. Feyenoord are top of the Dutch league after 11 matches, unbeaten, but they have one win and two defeats in Europe.

INTER.IT · NOV 1

The women beat Lazio 3–0, the Under 23 lose at Cosenza

Inter Women won 3–0 at the Arena Civica Gianni Brera on Sunday. Lazio turned the ball into their own net in the 22nd minute, and Haley Bugeja, the Maltese forward, scored in the 35th minute and again in the 58th. It was her 30th goal for the club, and no other foreign player has reached that mark.

Inter Women, under their head coach David Sassarini, have 13 points and five clean sheets in five league matches. The Under 23 side lost 1–0 at Cosenza in Serie C, beaten by a header from a corner in the 26th minute. They stay ninth with 17 points from 12 matches.

INTER.IT · NOV 1

Zielinski reaches 100 appearances

Piotr Zielinski, Inter’s Poland midfielder, made his 100th appearance for the club when he came on in the 72nd minute of the derby. He joined in 2024 and has 11 goals and nine assists.

INTER.IT · OCT 31

 
REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Pavard back with the group, and a stomach bug through derby week

Tuttosport reports that Pavard trained with the squad on Sunday and should be available in Rotterdam. Inter’s training gallery shows him on the field, which is as far as the club has gone.

The paper also says a stomach bug ran through the squad last week. By its account Marcus Thuram, the France forward, was the only attacker at full fitness for the derby. The forwards Francesco Pio Esposito and Ange-Yoan Bonny were weakened and used only late. Chivu’s first substitutions came in the 70th minute, two goals down, which the paper reads as a sign that others were short of fitness too.

UPDATE · TUTTOSPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · NOV 2

The lineup Tuttosport expects at De Kuip

Tuttosport expects a 3-5-2. Josep Martínez in goal, with Yann Bisseck, Manuel Akanji and Carlos Augusto across the back. Luis Henrique, Nicolò Barella, Hakan Calhanoglu, Piotr Zielinski and Federico Dimarco in midfield, Thuram and Esposito up front.

Inter have published no lineup of their own.
