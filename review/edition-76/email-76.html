Italy lose 4–1 in Brussels and miss the quarter-finals

Belgium beat Italy 4–1 on Sunday, so Italy finish third in their Nations League group. The Inter forward Francesco Pio Esposito put Italy ahead in the 11th minute and later missed a penalty.

It was his 10th goal in 15 games for Italy. Only Giuseppe Meazza reached ten at a younger age; Esposito is 21. The defender Alessandro Bastoni and the midfielder Nicolò Barella played all 90 minutes, and the defender Federico Dimarco came on at halftime.

The midfielders Andy Diouf and Hakan Çalhanoğlu met as France beat Türkiye. The midfielder Petar Sučić played 90 minutes for Croatia, and the defender Djed Spence played the last seven minutes for England.

INTER.IT · NOV 15

Juventus score three late to beat Inter Women

The forward Brittany Raphino scored twice for Inter Women against Juventus on Sunday, in the 54th and 74th minutes. Juventus scored three times from the 89th minute on and won 3–2.

It is Inter’s first league defeat of the season. They stay second in Serie A Women, five points behind Roma. The coach David Sassarini said “questo risultato brucia molto” — this result really stings. Inter visit Real Madrid in the Women’s Champions League on Wednesday.

INTER.IT · NOV 15

Tickets for Liverpool at San Siro go on sale this week

Inter host Liverpool in the Champions League on Tuesday, January 19, at 3:00 PM ET, 21:00 CET. Season-ticket holders can buy first, from Tuesday at 6:00 AM ET, 12:00 CET. General sale opens on Friday, November 20, at 6:00 AM ET, 12:00 CET. The limit is four tickets per person.

Lautaro could get half an hour at Atalanta

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy’s biggest sports daily, reports that the Inter captain Lautaro Martínez rejoins group training this week. QS, another Italian daily, says that is more than a month after he was hurt against Club Brugge. If all goes well, it expects about half an hour from him in Bergamo on Sunday.

GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT · QS VIA FCINTERNEWS · NOV 16

Zieliński stays with Poland as Akanji heads home

Gazzetta reports that the Inter midfielder Piotr Zieliński stayed with Poland and could play against Sweden in Wrocław on Tuesday. New today: Tuttosport, a Turin daily, says Inter’s doctors called him and he told them not to worry. The foot problem may still need managing.

The Inter defender Manuel Akanji left Switzerland’s camp with a muscle problem, as a precaution. Gazzetta says he could still make the trip to Bergamo.
