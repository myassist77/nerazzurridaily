Marcus Thuram and Aleksandar Stankovic have both left their national teams with muscle injuries, and the club says Ange-Yoan Bonny has a strain in his right hamstring.
NERAZZURRI DAILYEdition No. 71 · Wed, Nov 11 · View online

Manchester City scored four in Monza on Tuesday night to beat Inter Women 1–4. The defender Elisa Bartoli had put Inter ahead in the 24th minute, and Inter stay on four points in the Women’s Champions League. The break has also cost the men two more players: the forward Marcus Thuram and the midfielder Aleksandar Stankovic both left their national teams hurt.

CONFIRMED · CLUB · LEAGUE · UEFA

Bartoli scores, then City score four

Bartoli headed Inter in front in the 24th minute. The Manchester City forward Aoba Fujino answered in the 33rd minute. She scored again a minute before halftime. After the break the defender Kerstin Casparij made it 3–1 in the 56th minute, and the midfielder Grace Clinton added a fourth in the 70th.

David Sassarini, Inter’s head coach, did not argue with the gap. “Se dobbiamo distinguere il piano della qualità loro sono su un livello superiore” — on quality, they are at a higher level — he told the club’s channels.

The women are home to Juventus on Sunday at 9:00 AM ET (15:00 CET), at the Stadio Ernesto Breda. They go to Real Madrid on Wednesday, November 18 at 12:45 PM ET (18:45 CET).

SAID · INTER.IT · NOV 10

The club names Bonny’s injury

Inter said on Tuesday that the forward Ange-Yoan Bonny had tests at the Istituto Humanitas in Rozzano. They “revealed a muscle strain in his right hamstring,” the club said. Yesterday the injury was only a press report; this is the club naming it.

UPDATE · INTER.IT · NOV 10

 
REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Thuram and Stankovic leave their national teams

The French federation said on Tuesday that Thuram had withdrawn with a hamstring problem. It named the Bournemouth attacker Eli Junior Kroupi as his replacement. Thuram therefore misses France’s Nations League match against Italy at San Siro on Thursday at 2:45 PM ET. Four Inter players are in the Italy squad, and the midfielder Andy Diouf is still with France.

Serbia lost Stankovic the same day. He felt pain in his right groin after training on Monday, and the Serbian federation said tests found a muscle injury in his right thigh. He misses the Nations League matches against Germany and Greece.

Bonny is expected to be out about four weeks

FCInter1908 puts Bonny’s absence at about four weeks. It says the target is the Frosinone match on December 5. If that is too soon, the trip to Borussia Dortmund four days later is the next marker. The club has published no date.
