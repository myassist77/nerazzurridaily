Inter Women win the derby 3–0 at Milan

Haley Bugeja, an Inter forward, scored a penalty in the 56th minute and the Inter midfielder Benedetta Glionna made it two in the 68th. Bugeja added a second penalty in the 81st, and Milan’s goalkeeper was sent off in the 64th for handling outside her area.

Bugeja now has six league goals this season. Inter have 16 points from six matches and have not conceded a league goal. They host Manchester City in the Women’s Champions League on Tuesday at 3:00 PM ET (21:00 CET).

INTER.IT · NOV 7

Como arrive fourth after four straight wins

Cesc Fàbregas, in his fourth season as Como’s coach, brings a side that sits fourth in Serie A with 23 points. Como have won their last four league matches and have conceded eight goals, tied with Lazio for the fewest in the league.

The club’s analysis calls Nico Paz the focal point of Como’s attack, and says the striker Tasos Douvikas has six goals in four Champions League matches.

INTER.IT · NOV 8

Inter remember Sandro Mazzola on his birthday

Sandro Mazzola, the Grande Inter forward who spent 17 seasons at the club and died in September, would have turned 84 today. He made 565 competitive appearances and scored 161 goals.

“Inter is like a mother to me,” he said in his last interview on the club’s own channels.

Gazzetta expects Bastoni back in central defense

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy’s biggest sports daily, reports that Alessandro Bastoni, an Inter defender, returns to the middle of the defense today. He was suspended for the Champions League win at Feyenoord, and the paper says Cristian Chivu, Inter’s coach, will change little else from that lineup.

The reports disagree about the left. Gazzetta puts Carlos Augusto, an Inter defender, in the back line alongside the defender Manuel Akanji. Sky Sport Italia has him at left wing back, behind the left back Federico Dimarco.

UPDATE · GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTER1908 · NOV 8

Calhanoglu has told his agents he wants to stay

Gianluca Di Marzio, the Sky Sport Italia transfer reporter, said on his Caffè Di Marzio podcast that Hakan Calhanoglu, an Inter midfielder, wants to stay. He has told his agents to pursue a new contract, and the report describes an extension of at least two years.

His deal runs to June 30, 2027. Tuttosport reported on Wednesday that the agent Gordon Stipic was asking Inter for two years.
