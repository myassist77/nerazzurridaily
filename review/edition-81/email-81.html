Maresca will referee in Bergamo

Inter visit Atalanta at the Gewiss Stadium on Sunday at 2:45 PM ET, 20:45 CET. The referee is Fabio Maresca, from Naples. Inter’s match guide says they have won seven of their last eight league games against Atalanta and drawn the other.

The guide also notes that the striker Francesco Pio Esposito has scored in each of his last four games for Inter and Italy. No player on either side is suspended.

INTER.IT · NOV 20

Inter named team of the year in Lombardy

The Lombardy sports journalists’ association, GLGS-USSI Lombardia, has named Inter its team of the year, shared with Como. The club president, Giuseppe Marotta, collected the award at a ceremony in Milan.

“È un premio che riconosce il lavoro di tutta la società” — it is an award that recognizes the work of the whole club, he said.

SAID · INTER.IT · NOV 20

Inter Women visit Ternana on Sunday

Inter Women play Ternana away on Sunday at 6:30 AM ET, 12:30 CET, in round eight of the women’s Serie A, at the Stadio Moreno Gubbiotti in Narni. It is their first game since Wednesday’s 3–1 defeat at Real Madrid in the Champions League, which left them with four points from five games.

INTER.IT · NOV 21

 
REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Inter to travel on the day of the game

Sky Sport Italia says the squad will train on Saturday morning at Appiano Gentile, Inter’s training ground, then go home and leave for Bergamo only on Sunday. New today: Sky sees a choice in midfield between Petar Sučić and Henrikh Mkhitaryan.

It still expects Pio Esposito and the forward Marcus Thuram to start up front. The captain, Lautaro Martínez, trained with the group again, and the defender Manuel Akanji will be assessed with Wednesday’s Champions League game against Stuttgart in mind.
