Chivu wins the Panchina d’Oro

The Inter coach Cristian Chivu won the Panchina d’Oro, or Golden Bench, on Monday morning in Florence. The award goes to the best coach of the 2025/26 season, when Inter won the Double, the league and the Italian Cup, in his first year in charge.

Coaches across Italian soccer vote for the winner. “It’s always special to receive an award, especially when it’s my fellow coaches who choose the winner,” Chivu said.

Inter Women visit Real Madrid on Wednesday

Inter Women play Real Madrid in the Women’s Champions League on Wednesday, November 18, at 12:45 PM ET, 18:45 CET. The game is at the Estadio Alfredo Di Stéfano in Madrid. It is their first game since Sunday’s 3–2 home loss to Juventus.

Lautaro should rejoin the group this week

Sky Sport Italia reports that the Inter captain Lautaro Martínez, out for a month, should train with the group from Tuesday. The next two or three sessions will decide if he plays in Bergamo on Sunday or waits for Stuttgart.

New today: Corriere dello Sport says the defender John Stones, out since September 14, is fully available. The defender Manuel Akanji may need one or two more sessions. In Poland, the midfielder Piotr Zieliński trained without foot pain, according to the Polish site Przegląd Sportowy Onet.

Carlos Augusto close to a deal until 2031

The transfer reporter Fabrizio Romano says the defender Carlos Augusto has all but agreed a new contract to June 2031. Matteo Moretto, another transfer reporter, says it could be signed within a month.
