Inter beat VfB Stuttgart 2–0 and rise to 12th in the Champions League with nine points.
NERAZZURRI DAILYEdition No. 86 · Thu, Nov 26 · View online

Inter beat VfB Stuttgart 2–0 at San Siro on Wednesday. Petar Sučić, the midfielder, scored a penalty, and Lautaro Martínez, the captain, added a second off the bench. Alessandro Bastoni, the center back, went off before halftime, and Sky Sport Italia reports a sore left thigh.

Inter climb to 12th in Europe

The win takes Inter to nine points from five matches and 12th place in the Champions League table.

“That’s the target, but we’re taking it one game at a time,” Sučić said. Inter play Borussia Dortmund away on December 9.

INTER.IT · NOV 25

Chivu: “We did well in every aspect”

Cristian Chivu, Inter’s head coach, praised the team’s attitude. “The team has to remain together and not lose its unity,” he said.

Nicolò Barella, the midfielder, was named UEFA Player of the Match. “We showed character,” he said.

SAID · INTER.IT · NOV 25

Inter U23 lose 3–0 at Cerignola

Inter’s under-23 team, coached by Stefano Vecchi, lost 3–0 away to Audace Cerignola in Serie C, Italy’s third division. They are ninth with 23 points from 15 matches.

REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Bastoni a doubt for Genoa

Bastoni asked to come off in the 41st minute, and Federico Dimarco, the wing back, replaced him. Sky Sport Italia reports soreness in the quadriceps of his left thigh. He is due to have scans, and his place against Genoa on Saturday is at risk.
