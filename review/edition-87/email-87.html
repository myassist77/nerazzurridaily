Carlos Augusto extends to 2031

Inter announced on Thursday that Carlos Augusto will stay until June 30, 2031. He was 24 when he joined, and he has since won two Serie A titles, the Coppa Italia and the Italian Super Cup.

He told Inter TV, the club’s channel, that he was “molto felice” — very happy — and that he hopes to repay the club’s trust on the field.

Dortmund away tickets go on sale

Tickets for the away section at Borussia Dortmund on December 9 cost €18.50 each, standing only, one per person. Inter Club members can buy until 11:00 AM ET today, when the sale opens to the club’s Interista loyalty program.

Bastoni’s scans set for today

La Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy’s biggest sports daily, says the first diagnosis is soreness in the quadriceps, the large muscle at the front of the thigh. What is new: the scans are today, and the paper says they will show how serious it is. His place against Genoa on Saturday is still open.

UPDATE · GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · NOV 26

Zieliński and Bonny still train apart

FCInterNews, an Inter news site, reports that Piotr Zieliński, the midfielder, and Ange-Yoan Bonny, the striker, again trained apart from the squad at Appiano Gentile, Inter’s training ground. Hakan Çalhanoğlu, the midfielder, had treatment only, with the Super Cup match against Lazio on December 22 as his target.
