# The Debate No. 6 — The greatest Inter season: 1964–65, 2009–10 or 2023–24? (research, Oct 28, 2026)

All read this session. Authority order: inter.it > uefa.com > Wikipedia > press. Nothing disputed goes on a frame.

## 1964–65 — Helenio Herrera, the Grande Inter (OUR PICK)
- Serie A champions: 34 played, 22 W, 10 D, 2 L, 68 GF, 29 GA, 54 points (2 points per win); Milan second on 51 (3-point margin). — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964%E2%80%9365_Serie_A and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964%E2%80%9365_Inter_Milan_season (both give W22 D10 L2, 68–29, 54)
- Scudetto clinched June 6, 1965, last day, 2–2 v Torino at San Siro (Jair; Mazzola late penalty); 9th title; Inter had been 7 points behind Milan at one stage. — https://inter.it/en/news/6-june-1965-inter-9th-scudetto
- European Cup: Inter 1–0 Benfica, May 27, 1965, San Siro, Jair (inter.it says 42', Wikipedia and uefa.com say 43' — minute never on screen). Second consecutive European Cup ("third side to claim successive European Cups" — uefa.com). Semi: beat Liverpool 4–3 on aggregate after losing the first leg 3–1. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1965_European_Cup_final ; https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0258-0e6a04df66ce-acdf9fb5f934-1000--1964-65-jair-the-difference-for-inter ; https://www.inter.it/en/news/history-inter-champions-league-finals
- Intercontinental Cup 1964 (played inside the 1964–65 season): Independiente 1–0 Inter (Sept 9, 1964, Avellaneda); Inter 2–0 Independiente (Sept 23, 1964, San Siro); play-off Inter 1–0 Independiente a.e.t., Sept 26, 1964, Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid, Corso 110'. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964_Intercontinental_Cup ; https://www.inter.it/en/club/honours/coppa-intercontinentale (confirms the Madrid play-off and Corso 110')
- Coppa Italia: runners-up. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964%E2%80%9365_Inter_Milan_season
- Top scorer: Sandro Mazzola, 17 league goals. — Wikipedia season page and inter.it
- Era context: Herrera's Inter won the European Cup 1964 and 1965 plus both Intercontinental Cups, and Serie A 1962–63, 1964–65, 1965–66. — https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0253-0d7ff54821f7-e2055930d1e0-1000--the-greatest-teams-of-all-time-inter-1962-67
- Trophies in the season: 3 (Scudetto, European Cup, Intercontinental Cup) — champions of Italy, Europe and the world in the same season.

## 2009–10 — José Mourinho, the treble (THE COUNTER)
- Serie A champions: 38 played, 24 W, 10 D, 4 L, 75 GF, 34 GA, +41, 82 points; Roma second on 80 (2-point margin); final day May 16, 2010. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009%E2%80%9310_Serie_A and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009%E2%80%9310_Inter_Milan_season
- Coppa Italia: Inter 1–0 Roma, May 5, 2010, Stadio Olimpico, Milito. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Coppa_Italia_final
- Champions League: Bayern 0–2 Inter, May 22, 2010, Madrid, Milito 35' and 70'. — https://www.inter.it/en/news/history-inter-champions-league-finals ; Wikipedia season page
- Supercoppa Italiana 2009: lost 2–1 to Lazio. — Wikipedia season page
- Top scorer: Diego Milito, 22 Serie A goals (30 in all competitions). — Wikipedia season page
- Trophies in the season: 3 (Serie A, Coppa Italia, Champions League) — the only treble by an Italian club. The Club World Cup came in December 2010, i.e. the 2010–11 season, so it is not counted here.

## 2023–24 — Simone Inzaghi, the second star (THE THIRD CANDIDATE)
- Serie A champions: 38 played, 29 W, 7 D, 2 L, 89 GF, 22 GA, +67, 94 points; Milan second on 75 (19-point margin); clinched April 22, 2024, 2–1 v Milan in the derby (Acerbi, Thuram), five games to spare; 20th title → the second star. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Serie_A ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Inter_Milan_season ; https://www.inter.it/en/news/inter-recap-2024-second-star (94 points, 89 scored, 22 conceded, best attack and best defence, Sommer 19 clean sheets)
- Supercoppa Italiana: Inter 1–0 Napoli, Jan 22, 2024, Riyadh, Lautaro Martínez stoppage-time winner; third in a row. — Wikipedia season page and inter.it recap
- Coppa Italia: out in the round of 16, 1–2 a.e.t. v Bologna, Dec 20, 2023. Champions League: out in the round of 16 v Atlético Madrid on penalties (1–0 home, 1–2 away, 2–3 shoot-out, March 13, 2024). — Wikipedia season page
- Top scorer: Lautaro Martínez, 24 Serie A goals (capocannoniere, Lega Serie A MVP). — inter.it recap
- Trophies in the season: 2 (Serie A, Supercoppa Italiana). Not Inter's points record (97 in 2006–07, from the D04 record) — so "record" is not claimed.
