Six Inter players close out the break

Belgium host Italy in Brussels at 2:45 PM ET, 20:45 CET. Four Inter players are in the squad: the midfielder Nicolò Barella, the defenders Alessandro Bastoni and Federico Dimarco, and the forward Francesco Pio Esposito.

Italy lead the group with 10 points, level with France and one point ahead of Belgium. Sky Sport Italia says not losing is enough; if Italy lose, they need France to lose as well.

Two more Inter players meet in that Bordeaux match: the midfielder Andy Diouf for France and the midfielder Hakan Çalhanoğlu for Türkiye. The midfielder Petar Sučić is with Croatia and the defender Djed Spence with England. Those matches also kick off at 2:45 PM ET.

Inter Women host Juventus at the Breda

Inter Women play Juventus at 9:00 AM ET, 15:00 CET, in matchday 7 of Serie A Women. It is at the Stadio Ernesto Breda in Sesto San Giovanni, not the usual Arena Civica. Entry is free.

New since yesterday: Inter are second in Serie A Women with 16 points, Juventus fifth with 9. In Italy it is on Rai 2, RaiPlay, DAZN and Raisport. There is no United States listing yet.

UPDATE · INTER.IT · NOV 14

Japan beat Brazil with Carlos Augusto at left back

Japan beat Brazil 3–2 in a friendly in Singapore on Saturday. The Inter defender Carlos Augusto played all 90 minutes at left back, and Tatsuhiro Sakamoto won it in stoppage time.

Zieliński sat out Romania with a foot problem

The Inter midfielder Piotr Zieliński sat out Poland’s Nations League match at Romania on Saturday. Mateusz Borek of Kanał Sportowy, a Polish outlet, reported pain in the sole of his foot. It made running hard.

Late on Saturday, FCInterNews reported from the Polish federation’s own website that Poland cannot say how long he will be out. His place against Sweden on Tuesday is not certain. Inter play at Atalanta next Sunday.

KANAŁ SPORTOWY VIA FCINTERNEWS · NOV 14

Fenerbahçe enter the Çalhanoğlu picture

The Turkish daily Sabah reported on Saturday that Fenerbahçe are ready to bid €10 million for the Inter midfielder Hakan Çalhanoğlu. Yesterday’s item had Juventus; Fenerbahçe is the new name.

Çalhanoğlu is 32 and his contract runs out in June. Inter have offered him a new deal. The report says he is not happy with it.
