Inter beat Como 2–0 at San Siro on Sunday. The forwards Pio Esposito and Marcus Thuram scored in the 12th and the 19th, and Inter now have 28 points from 11 matches, a point behind Lazio. Serie A stops for the international break, so the next Inter match of any kind is the women’s Champions League match against Manchester City on Tuesday.

Chivu counts nine matches in 27 days

Cristian Chivu, Inter’s coach, spoke to the club’s own channels after the final whistle. “Abbiamo giocato nove partite in 27 giorni” — we have played nine matches in 27 days. He said the team came through them with ups and downs, in both quality of play and results.

He credited the win to tempo: “oggi abbiamo alzato ritmo e intensità” — today we raised the tempo and the intensity. On the derby defeat: “ci siamo rimboccati le maniche” — we rolled up our sleeves. He would not read the table yet. “L’importante è come si arriva a marzo” — what matters is where you stand in March.

SAID · INTER.IT · NOV 8

Dimarco reaches 250 appearances for Inter

Federico Dimarco, Inter’s left wing back, made his 250th appearance for the club against Como. He first played for the first team on December 11, 2014, coming on in the second half at Qarabag.

He has won two Serie A titles, three Coppa Italia trophies and three Italian Super Cups. His 18 assists in Serie A last season are a single-season record for the league, and he was named its most valuable player for 2025-26.

INTER.IT · NOV 8

Inter Women host Manchester City in Monza on Tuesday

Inter Women play Manchester City in the Women’s Champions League on Tuesday at 3:00 PM ET, which is 21:00 CET. The match is at the Brianteo Stadium in Monza, not San Siro, and the club has not listed a United States broadcaster.

Inter are eighth of the 18 teams in the league phase with four points from three matchdays. The top four go straight to the quarterfinals and the next eight into a playoff round.

Romano says Inter want to open talks with Thuram’s agent

Fabrizio Romano, the transfer reporter, said on his YouTube channel that Inter want to sit down with Meïssa N’diaye. N’diaye is Thuram’s agent, and the club wants to map out a new contract. Romano called it preparation rather than anything urgent.

The same report says the renewal of Carlos Augusto, an Inter defender, is close. Inter have already exercised their option on Dimarco, it says, but still plan to talk to his representatives. No figures were given.
