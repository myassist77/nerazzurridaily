Inter Women play Manchester City at the Stadio Brianteo in Monza today, kickoff at 3:00 PM ET (21:00 CET). It is the fourth of six league-phase matches in the Women’s Champions League, and Inter sit eighth of eighteen on four points. With Serie A stopped, thirteen Inter players are away with their national teams.

CONFIRMED · CLUB · LEAGUE · UEFA

Inter go in on four points, City on three

Inter’s four points came from a 1–0 win over BK Häcken, a 1–1 draw at Austria Wien and a 6–0 defeat at OL Lyonnes. Manchester City have drawn all three of theirs.

David Sassarini, Inter’s head coach, spoke to the club’s own channels. He wants “leggerezza quando avremo la palla e voglia di divertirsi” — lightness on the ball and a wish to enjoy ourselves.

UEFA lists the match in the United States on Paramount+ and ESPN Deportes.

SAID · INTER.IT · NOV 9

Thirteen Nerazzurri are away with their national teams

Italy called up four: the midfielder Nicolò Barella, the defender Alessandro Bastoni, the wing back Federico Dimarco and the forward Francesco Pio Esposito. All four could face two teammates on Thursday, when Italy play France in Milan at 2:45 PM ET (20:45 CET) in the Nations League.

The forward Marcus Thuram and the midfielder Andy Diouf are in that France squad. Seven more are away: the defender Carlos Augusto with Brazil, the midfielder Petar Sucic with Croatia, the defender Djed Spence with England and the midfielder Piotr Zielinski with Poland. The midfielder Aleksandar Stankovic is with Serbia, the defender Manuel Akanji with Switzerland and the midfielder Hakan Calhanoglu with Turkey.

INTER.IT · NOV 9

Inter U23’s match with Audace Cerignola is postponed

The Serie C match was set for Saturday, November 14, inside the international break. It has been moved to Wednesday, November 25 at 8:30 AM ET (14:30 CET).

REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Bonny drops out of the Ivory Coast squad

The forward Ange-Yoan Bonny is out of Ivory Coast’s squad for two Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Gambia, on November 12 and November 17. Football Italia reports that the national team announced it in a statement, and that Bonny was hurt in Sunday’s win over Como. Oumar Diakité of Sturm Graz takes his place.

FC Inter 1908 calls it a muscle injury and says he has stayed in Milan for tests.

IVORY COAST NATIONAL TEAM VIA FOOTBALL ITALIA · NOV 9

Gazzetta says Inter want Calhanoglu signed before the new year

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy’s biggest sports daily, says Inter want a new deal with the midfielder Hakan Calhanoglu. They want it signed before the end of the year. His contract runs out in June 2027.

Corriere dello Sport reported on Monday that talks are at an early stage. The player is not against staying, it said, but the money is not settled.
