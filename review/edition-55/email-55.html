Lautaro Martínez is on the Ballon d'Or shortlist

Inter said Lautaro Martínez, the club captain, is one of 30 players on the shortlist for the 2026 Ballon d'Or. France Football gives the prize to the men's player of the year, and this is Martínez's fifth nomination. The club said the ceremony is tonight at the London Palladium, the first time it has been held in London, and it published no start time.

Martínez finished seventh in the 2024 vote, his best placing. Last season he won the Serie A title and the Coppa Italia, Italy's domestic cup, and was the league's top scorer with 17 goals.

Ivan Provedel makes his Inter debut

Ivan Provedel started in goal against Fiorentina, his first appearance for the club. Inter said he completed 30 of his 31 passes and, with his side a goal down, saved a close-range shot from the Fiorentina substitute Gnonto in the 79th minute. The long-range goal he had conceded denied him a clean sheet.

Corriere dello Sport expects Jones and Pavard back for Feyenoord

Corriere dello Sport reported that Curtis Jones, the midfielder, and Benjamin Pavard, the center back, both have adductor trouble in the right thigh. The paper says both could rejoin team training after Wednesday's match at Venezia, which makes the Champions League match at Feyenoord on November 3 their likely return. It calls the derby against AC Milan on October 31 unlikely.

The same report puts John Stones, the England defender, on course for Como on November 8. Lautaro Martínez and Luis Henrique, the forward, will be available only after the international break, it says, with Atalanta on November 22 the target.

CORRIERE DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · OCT 25

Gazzetta says Dimarco's new deal is stuck on wages

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy's biggest sports daily, reported that Federico Dimarco, the Inter left back, is asking for about €7.5 million a season. He earns about €5.2 million now. Italian salaries are quoted after tax. The raise would put him just behind Lautaro Martínez in the wage order.

The paper says Inter are in no hurry, because his contract runs to 2027 and the club can extend it to 2028 on its own. Oaktree, Inter's owner, considers a salary that size hard to carry.
