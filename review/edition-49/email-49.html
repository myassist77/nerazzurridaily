INTER.IT · OCT 19

Inter’s own scouting report finds Shakhtar vulnerable in transition

Arda Turan is in his second season as Shakhtar’s head coach. Inter’s analysis has his side top of the Ukrainian league, with eight wins from nine and twenty goals scored. In the Champions League their expected goals — a measure of chance quality — fall from 1.98 to 0.94, and possession drops from roughly 65 percent to 50 percent.

Inter name the weakness plainly. When Shakhtar lose the ball with players committed forward, gaps open behind their full-backs and between defense and midfield. PSV Eindhoven punished them that way in September.

INTER.IT · OCT 19

Haley Bugeja becomes Inter Women’s leading foreign scorer

Bugeja, a Maltese forward who arrived in Milan in 2023, scored in the 39th minute of Sunday’s 3–0 win over Parma. She is now the highest-scoring foreign player in Inter Women’s history, with twenty-nine goals.

Napoli Women are next, away, on Saturday, October 24.

REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Gazzetta has Bonny starting and the goalkeeper undecided

What has changed since Monday is the goalkeeper. Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy’s biggest sports daily, no longer has Ivan Provedel down to start, and says Chivu will choose between him and Josep Martinez only shortly before kickoff. Provedel has yet to play a competitive match for Inter.

The rest of the projected lineup: Ange-Yoan Bonny takes his first start of the season ahead of Francesco Pio Esposito, with Marcus Thuram beside him. Hakan Calhanoglu returns as the deep-lying playmaker, Nicolò Barella next to him, and either Piotr Zielinski or Henrikh Mkhitaryan third.

UPDATE · GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · OCT 20

Corriere dello Sport: without Lautaro, Thuram has to score in Europe

Lautaro Martínez, the captain, is out injured. Tuesday’s Corriere dello Sport reports that the weight in Europe now falls on Thuram, who has no Champions League goal this season. His last came in September 2025, when he scored twice away at Ajax.

The paper expects Lautaro back after the international break, for Atalanta and then Stuttgart.
