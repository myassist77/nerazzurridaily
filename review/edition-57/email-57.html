Inter go to the Penzo with top spot to protect

Kickoff at the Stadio Pier Luigi Penzo is 1:30 PM ET, 18:30 CET, in matchday 9 of Serie A.

Inter have seven wins and a draw from eight matches and lead on goal difference from Lazio. Venezia are bottom with three points, one win and seven defeats, under Davide Nicola, who replaced Giovanni Stroppa as head coach on October 14.

INTER.IT · OCT 28

The Under-23 side beat Scafatese 2–1 at the Breda

Jan Zuberek, on as a substitute in the 19th minute, put Inter's Under-23 side ahead in the 36th, and Palmieri equalized for Scafatese in the 39th. Mattia Marello won it in the 62nd with a left-footed free kick that went in off the post. The rescheduled Serie C match was played Tuesday at the Breda.

It was the first home league win since September 20 for Stefano Vecchi's side, and Zuberek has scored in four straight matches.

INTER.IT · OCT 27

Inter Women are at OL Lyonnes in the Champions League

Kickoff at the OL Stadium in Décines-Charpieu is 4:00 PM ET, 21:00 CET, in matchday 3 of the Women's Champions League league phase. Inter have four points from two matches, a 1–0 win over BK Häcken and a 1–1 draw at Austria Wien. OL Lyonnes have won the competition eight times. Twelve teams advance from the league phase.

REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Sky Sport Italia has Stankovic starting and Calhanoglu rested

Sky Sport Italia has now named a full projected lineup, where yesterday it said only that the forward Marcus Thuram might rest. It keeps Ivan Provedel in goal, with Alessandro Bastoni back in the middle of a back three beside Yann Aurel Bisseck and Carlos Augusto. Federico Dimarco returns on the left, and Aleksandar Stankovic joins Nicolò Barella and Piotr Zielinski in midfield. Hakan Calhanoglu rests and Manuel Akanji starts on the bench.

Ange-Yoan Bonny and Francesco Pio Esposito are the favored pair up front, and Thuram's place is still open.

UPDATE · SKY SPORT ITALIA VIA FCINTERNEWS · OCT 27

Lautaro Martínez is still out, with his recovery on schedule

FCInterNews reports that Lautaro Martínez, Inter's captain and center forward, ran alone at Appiano Gentile, Inter's training ground, on Tuesday, with his recovery going to plan. He has been out since October 15 with a left hamstring strain, and is not expected back until after the November international break.
