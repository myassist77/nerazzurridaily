Four Inter men with Italy, one with France

Inter said on Wednesday that 11 of their players had left for national teams. Four are in the Italy squad for tonight: the midfielder Nicolò Barella, the defenders Alessandro Bastoni and Federico Dimarco, and the forward Francesco Pio Esposito. The midfielder Andy Diouf is in the France squad.

A sixth Inter man is in the same group. The midfielder Hakan Çalhanoğlu is with Turkey, who play Belgium in Izmir tonight at 12:00 PM ET, 18:00 CET. Italy close the group away to Belgium on Sunday.

INTER.IT · NOV 11

Inter Women host Juventus on Sunday, and entry is free

Inter Women play Juventus at 9:00 AM ET, 15:00 CET on Sunday, in matchday 7 of Serie A Women. It is at the Stadio Ernesto Breda in Sesto San Giovanni, not their usual Arena Civica. The club says entry is free.

In Italy it is live on DAZN and Raisport, and free on Rai 2 and RaiPlay. No United States broadcaster is published.

REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Thuram’s tests reportedly show no tear

Yesterday’s edition reported that the forward Marcus Thuram had left the France squad. What is new is the result. La Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy’s biggest sports daily, and Sky Sport Italia report that his tests showed a minor muscle problem in the hamstring and no tear. Football Italia says he hopes to play when Serie A resumes at Atalanta on November 22.

France are also missing the forward Kylian Mbappé. Their head coach, Zinedine Zidane, said on Wednesday that Mbappé is hurt and will not be risked. On Inter’s side the forward Ange-Yoan Bonny withdrew from Ivory Coast, and the midfielder Aleksandar Stankovic left the Serbia camp.

UPDATE · GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FOOTBALL ITALIA · NOV 11

Carlos Augusto’s renewal is said to be stuck on money

The journalist Nicolò Schira reported on Tuesday that pay is holding up the defender Carlos Augusto’s new contract. His agents are asking for up to €5 million a year (Italian salaries are quoted after tax). Schira says Inter are working toward an extension to 2031.

FCInterNews, which ran the report, ties the demand to a tax break for players who move to Italy from abroad. Inter have announced nothing.
