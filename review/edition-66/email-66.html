CONFIRMED · CLUB · LEAGUE · UEFA

England call Spence up for Croatia and Spain

Djed Spence, Inter's England defender, is in the 25-man squad the Football Association named on Friday. England play Croatia at Wembley and then Spain in Madrid. The announcement says Spence returns after missing the last camp with an injury.

He is the only player in the squad who plays for an Italian club.

ENGLAND FOOTBALL · NOV 6

UEFA name Bisseck in the team of the week

Yann Bisseck, Inter's center-back, is one of eleven players UEFA's Technical Observer Group chose from Champions League Matchday 4. UEFA credited his first goal in the competition, the opener at Feyenoord, and a defensive performance that helped Inter keep a clean sheet.

Tasos Douvikas, the Como striker Inter face on Sunday, is in the same eleven.

UEFA.COM · NOV 5

Inter appoint a director of football development

Simone Lo Schiavo joined Inter on Thursday as Director of Football Development, reporting to Dario Baccin, the club's chief football officer. He started at Vibonese, was head of scouting and then youth technical coordinator at Roma, ran scouting at Salernitana, and most recently worked in Palermo's scouting department.

Giuseppe Marotta, Inter's president and chief executive, said Lo Schiavo brings "valuable expertise and great enthusiasm" to the club. Marotta added that he will play an important role in the daily work of the football department.

Sky Sport Italia expects Esposito to start next to Thuram

Sky Sport Italia's page for Sunday has Cristian Chivu, Inter's head coach, playing a 3-5-2 with two forwards, Francesco Pio Esposito and Marcus Thuram. Djed Spence and Federico Dimarco are the wing-backs.

The same page marks Lautaro Martínez, Inter's captain, and Luis Henrique, a winger, as unavailable. Curtis Jones, a midfielder, and John Stones, a defender, are listed as doubts and named among the substitutes.

SKY SPORT ITALIA · NOV 6

Corriere dello Sport counts 20 goals in six meetings

Inter and Como have scored 20 goals between them across their last six meetings, an average of 3.33 a match, Corriere dello Sport reports. Inter won five of those six and drew the other.

In each of the last two, a 4–3 in Serie A and a 3–2 in the Coppa Italia semifinal, Inter came back from two goals down.
