Carlos Augusto won it in the 77th minute. Ten-man Inter beat Shakhtar Donetsk 1–0 at San Siro, their first Champions League win of the season. Alessandro Bastoni, the center-back, had been sent off nine minutes earlier for two yellow cards in two minutes. Josep Martinez, the goalkeeper, saved twice in the 88th minute, and the three points lift Inter to 20th of the 36 teams in the league phase.

Chivu: “We showed that we can dig deep”

“We became a little anxious,” said Cristian Chivu, Inter's head coach. He put that down to the two European defeats that came before it. “We showed that we can dig deep,” he said of the second.

He called the win hugely important, and said his three substitutions in the 59th minute brought fresh energy. He also backed his goalkeepers: he has “complete faith” in all of them.

SAID · INTER.IT · OCT 21

Martinez: “Winning like this with ten men means so much”

Josep Martinez made three important saves: a header in the first half, then a shot and the follow-up header in the 88th minute. “Winning like this with ten men means so much,” the goalkeeper said.

Manuel Akanji, the defender, was less happy with the officials. “I don't understand how the referee could have shown two yellow cards,” he said. Nicolò Barella, the midfielder who wore the armband, said what mattered was winning, no matter how.

SAID · INTER.IT · OCT 21

Inter's under-20s beat Shakhtar 2–0 for a first Youth League win

Five hours before kickoff, Inter's under-20 side beat Shakhtar 2–0 in the UEFA Youth League. Alen Vukaj scored in first-half stoppage time and Christian Carrara in the 75th minute. It was a first league-phase win for Emiliano Bigica's team.

Four still out for Sunday, and no Lautaro Martinez until after the break

Corriere dello Sport reports that four players will miss Fiorentina on Sunday and Venezia on Wednesday. They are Lautaro Martinez, the forward, Benjamin Pavard and John Stones, both defenders, and Curtis Jones, the midfielder.

The paper has Pavard and Jones close to returning, perhaps for Como on November 8. Stones, out since September 14, is in contention for the derby on October 31. Lautaro Martinez is not expected back before the break.

CORRIERE DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · OCT 22

Mariani will referee Sunday's match, and Florence is already unhappy

Sky Sport Italia published the appointments for the eighth matchday of Serie A. The referee Mariani will take Inter against Fiorentina on Sunday, with Fabbri on VAR.

La Nazione, the Florence daily, reports that Fiorentina found the league's account of the refereeing in their Como match “non soddisfacenti e non esaustive” — not satisfactory and not exhaustive. Florence still fears mistakes, even though Fiorentina have won 11 of the 19 matches Mariani has refereed.
