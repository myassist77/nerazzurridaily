Inter and AC Milan unveiled the Generali Stadium on Thursday evening at the Castello Sforzesco. It holds 71,500, construction starts in 2028, and the clubs are targeting the 2031/32 season for the first match. The two meet at San Siro on Saturday, with Inter top of Serie A, Milan fifth, and Lautaro Martínez one of five Inter players the papers say will miss it.

Inter and Milan unveiled the Generali Stadium

The clubs presented the project in the Courtyard of Arms at the Castello Sforzesco, in Milan. The architects are Foster + Partners and MANICA, led by Lord Norman Foster and David Manica. Generali takes the naming rights and becomes official insurance partner of both clubs.

The stadium holds about 71,500 in two tiers that run in an unbroken bowl around the field, with the end stands raked at up to 37 degrees. It anchors a 281,000-square-meter district of parks, shops and public space, backed by the clubs' owners, Oaktree and RedBird Capital Partners.

The derby kicks off at 3:45 PM ET on Saturday

AC Milan host Inter at San Siro in Serie A matchday 10. Kickoff is 3:45 PM ET, 20:45 CET. Paramount+ and Telemundo carry it in the United States.

Inter lead Serie A with 25 points from nine matches. Milan are fifth with 18, after a 2–4 home defeat by Bologna on Wednesday. Ruben Amorim, Milan's head coach since the summer, plays a 3-4-3. In 246 derbies Inter have won 91 and Milan 84.

Inter Women host Lazio on Sunday, free entry

Inter Women play Lazio at the Arena Civica Gianni Brera in Milan on Sunday at 6:30 AM ET, 12:30 CET, in matchday 5 of Serie A Women. Admission is free.

Inter, coached by David Sassarini, are second with 10 points, two behind Roma. They drew 0–0 at Napoli in their last league match, a fourth straight game without conceding.

Papers put Lautaro Martínez among five absentees

Corriere dello Sport reports that Lautaro Martínez, Inter's captain and center-forward, will not be fit for the derby. The paper also rules out the defenders Benjamin Pavard and John Stones, the midfielder Curtis Jones and the forward Luis Henrique. Inter have published no team news.

It expects Josep Martínez to return in goal and Pio Esposito to start alongside Marcus Thuram, with Nicolò Barella and Hakan Çalhanoğlu in midfield. Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy's biggest sports daily, agrees, and calls the rest of the defense a "rebus" — a puzzle. Cristian Chivu, Inter's head coach, speaks to reporters at 9:30 AM ET today.

CORRIERE DELLO SPORT · OCT 30

Calcio e Finanza puts the naming rights at €650 million

Calcio e Finanza, the Italian football-business site, reports that Generali will pay €650 million over 25 years for the stadium name — about €26 million a season. Neither club has published a figure. For comparison, the site puts Juventus's Allianz Stadium deal at €103.1 million from 2023 to 2030.
