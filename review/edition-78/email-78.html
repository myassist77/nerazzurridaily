Inter Women face Real Madrid today

Inter Women play Real Madrid in Madrid today at 12:45 PM ET, 18:45 CET, in round five of the Women’s Champions League league phase. Inter are 13th with four points, two behind Real Madrid.

The Inter Women coach David Sassarini said “Ci aspetta una partita di grande fascino” — a fascinating game awaits us. “Abbiamo bisogno di punti,” he added — we need points.

UPDATE · INTER.IT · NOV 17

Tickets on sale for Lazio and Liverpool

Inter play Lazio in the Supercoppa, the Italian Super Cup, at San Siro on Tuesday, December 22, at 3:00 PM ET, 21:00 CET. It is a single game. Sales opened today for season ticket holders, and the general sale starts on December 2.

Tickets for the Champions League home game against Liverpool on Tuesday, January 19, at 3:00 PM ET, 21:00 CET, are also on sale. The general sale opens on Friday.

INTER.IT · NOV 17 · NOV 18

Zieliński captains Poland

The midfielder Piotr Zieliński captained Poland in a 1–1 draw with Sweden in Wroclaw on Tuesday. He came off after 81 minutes.

REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Lautaro and Thuram back with the group

Sky Sport Italia reports that the Inter captain Lautaro Martínez and the striker Marcus Thuram trained with the group on Tuesday at Appiano Gentile, Inter’s training ground. It was Lautaro’s first session with the group since his injury. Both should be in the squad in Bergamo on Sunday.

UPDATE · SKY SPORT ITALIA VIA FCINTERNEWS · NOV 17

Lautaro may start on the bench

Tuttosport says the coach Cristian Chivu will not rush Lautaro, and that Thuram is further along and could start next to the striker Francesco Pio Esposito. Corriere dello Sport, which notes Lautaro has missed seven games, also expects one of the two to start on the bench.
