CONFIRMED · CLUB · LEAGUE · UEFA

Luis Henrique has a strained left adductor

Luis Henrique had tests on Friday morning at the Humanitas Institute in Rozzano. The club’s statement says they showed “un risentimento all’adduttore lungo della coscia sinistra” — a strain of the long adductor muscle in the left thigh.

Inter set no return date, and did not say whether he can play on Sunday. The club’s matchday guide lists no suspensions for either team.

INTER.IT · OCT 23

Mariani referees, and the gates open two hours before kickoff

The club’s matchday guide names Maurizio Mariani as the referee. Kickoff is 7:30 AM ET, 12:30 CET, in Serie A matchday 8. It is only the second Serie A meeting between these clubs at that hour. The first was a 0–0 draw at the Franchi in April 2012.

Gates open at 5:30 AM ET, 10:30 CET, and the Inter Live Show runs beside the field until kickoff. Inter are level with Lazio at the top, with six wins and a draw from seven matches. The club counts 17 Serie A matches unbeaten under Cristian Chivu, its head coach.

INTER.IT · OCT 23

Both the women and the U23 side play on Saturday

The women’s kickoff is now published. They are away to Napoli at 10:00 AM ET, 16:00 CEST, a week after beating Parma 3–0. Inter U23 host Sorrento at 8:30 AM ET, 14:30 CEST, at the Stadio Ernesto Breda.

UPDATE · INTER.IT · OCT 23

 
REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Gazzetta expects as many as five changes

Gazzetta dello Sport reports that Cristian Chivu plans at least four changes among the field players, on top of the switch in goal. The defenders Yann Bisseck, Manuel Akanji and Alessandro Bastoni are expected in the back three. Carlos Augusto, the left-sided defender, would take the place of Federico Dimarco.

The same report puts Piotr Zielinski, the midfielder, in contention after his substitute appearance against Shakhtar Donetsk. Francesco Pio Esposito would return in attack in place of Yoan Bonny, the forward. Sky Sport Italia said on Friday evening that Ivan Provedel, the goalkeeper, will make his first Inter appearance.

GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · OCT 24

Chivu’s substitutes have five goals and four assists

Corriere dello Sport counts five goals and four assists from Inter players who came off the bench in the season’s first ten matches. Bonny has three of the goals. Carlos Augusto has one goal and two of the assists. He scored the winner against Shakhtar Donetsk, set up by Henrikh Mkhitaryan off the bench.
