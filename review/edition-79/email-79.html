Inter Women lose in Madrid

Inter Women lost 3–1 to Real Madrid in heavy rain on Wednesday, in round five of the Women’s Champions League league phase. Real Madrid led when the midfielder Karolina Vilhjalmsdottir was sent off after a video review in the 41st minute.

The striker Michela Cambiaghi tied the game in first-half stoppage time, after a corner from Benedetta Glionna. Real Madrid scored twice late, the second from a penalty. Inter stay on four points and host Arsenal on December 16 in their last league-phase game, with a place in the knockout rounds still possible.

The coach David Sassarini said “In undici contro undici abbiamo giocato alla pari” — eleven against eleven, we were their equals.

Pio Esposito scores three for Italy

The striker Francesco Pio Esposito scored twice in Italy’s 3–1 win over France and once in a 4–1 loss to Belgium. He now has ten goals in 15 games for Italy.

The midfielder Petar Sučić scored for Croatia in a 2–1 loss to England at Wembley, and the wing-back Federico Dimarco set up a goal against France.

INTER.IT · NOV 18

Inter’s new foundation starts soon

Inter say Fondazione Inter, the club’s new foundation, will start work in the coming weeks. It brings together the club’s community and social work in Milan, with a focus on young people and sport. Inter Campus, the club’s charity program since 1997, stays part of the plan.

INTER.IT · NOV 18

 
REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Inter open renewal talks with Çalhanoğlu

Corriere dello Sport says Inter are willing to offer the midfielder Hakan Çalhanoğlu, whose contract runs to 2027, a new deal not far from his current salary. The fixed part would be lower and the bonuses richer, tied to trophies, Champions League progress and games played. The paper says talks are still open.
