Benjamin Pavard is already back on the field, and Gazzetta dello Sport expects Lautaro Martínez to return only after the international break.
NERAZZURRI DAILYEdition No. 65 · Thu, Nov 5 · View online

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy’s biggest sports daily, reports that Curtis Jones and John Stones trained with their teammates on Wednesday. Jones, a midfielder, and Stones, a defender, are both England internationals and should be in the squad for Como at San Siro on Sunday. Lautaro Martínez, Inter’s captain, has a muscle problem in his left thigh and is not expected back before the international break.

Pavard plays his first minutes since the injury

Benjamin Pavard, the France defender, came on in the 85th minute at De Kuip, his first appearance since his injury. Inter kept a second straight clean sheet.

The squad trained on Wednesday at Appiano Gentile, Inter’s training ground, under Cristian Chivu, the head coach. The club’s pictures from the session show Jones and Stones both on the field. Como visit San Siro on Sunday at 12:00 PM ET, 18:00 CET, in matchday 11 of Serie A.

INTER.IT · NOV 4

Spence takes his first player of the match award

UEFA named Djed Spence, the England wing-back, player of the match at De Kuip, his first such award at Inter. His pass for the defender Yann Bisseck’s opener in the eighth minute was his first assist in an Inter shirt.

Spence told the club he was delighted with the win and the three points, and that he is settling in well. “We need to show the same mentality and hunger on Sunday against Como if we want to get another win,” he said.

SAID · INTER.IT · NOV 3

Inter mourn a Tenerife player

The club joined the football community in mourning the death of Jesús de Miguel, a CD Tenerife player, and sent its condolences to his family, his club and his teammates. Inter gave no further detail.

Gazzetta says Jones and Stones are available for Como

Both men had days of individual sessions on the field after what the paper calls nagging injuries, which it does not name. It reports that they return to full group work from Thursday and should be in the squad on Sunday.

Lautaro’s thigh problem is the most stubborn in the squad, the paper says, and the target is Atalanta away on November 22, after the international break. Luis Henrique, the Brazil winger, is also still training alone.

Chiffi is appointed for Inter against Como

Daniele Chiffi, a referee from Padua, will take charge of Sunday’s match, according to the matchday 11 appointments carried by Italpress, an Italian news agency. The rest of the team of officials was not listed.
