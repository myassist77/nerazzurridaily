Inter Women won 4–0 at Ternana and stay second in Serie A Women.

Francesco Pio Esposito scored three times in Bergamo. But Inter drew 4–4 with Atalanta after leading by two goals on three occasions. The tying goal was a penalty in stoppage time. Inter Women won 4–0 at Ternana, and VfB Stuttgart visit San Siro on Wednesday.

Chivu: “two points thrown away”

Cristian Chivu, Inter’s head coach, was blunt on the club’s website. “For me, that’s two points thrown away, but we have to learn from it.” He added: “We’re conceding too many goals for a team with our ambitions.”

Esposito, the striker, scored his first Serie A hat-trick. “Even after scoring three goals, it’s hard to be happy,” he said.

SAID · INTER.IT · NOV 22

Inter Women win 4–0 at Ternana

Inter Women beat Ternana 4–0 in Narni on Sunday. Masa Tomasevic scored in the 18th minute, Michela Cambiaghi in the 43rd, Benedetta Glionna in the 49th and Brittany Raphino in stoppage time.

Inter are second in Serie A Women with 19 points, five behind Roma. After the international break they visit Roma on Saturday, December 12, at 9:00 AM ET, 15:00 CET.

INTER.IT · NOV 22

Stuttgart at San Siro on Wednesday

Inter host VfB Stuttgart in the Champions League on Wednesday at 3:00 PM ET, 21:00 CET. Chivu speaks to the press on Tuesday at 8:30 AM ET, 14:30 CET, at Appiano Gentile, Inter’s training ground.

Çalhanoğlu may have a hamstring injury

The club confirms only that Hakan Çalhanoğlu, the midfielder, went off injured about half an hour in. Sportmediaset reports a muscle problem in the back of his right thigh.

Tuttosport, a Turin sports daily, says tests will show how bad it is, and that he risks missing the rest of 2026.
