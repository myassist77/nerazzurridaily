## Javier Zanetti (Inter 1995–2014, No. 4)
- 858 appearances, 21 goals, 16 trophies (stats box); "160 European appearances, 47 derbies, 13 years as captain and most importantly, 16 winners' medals"; arrived 1995; first trophy the 1998 UEFA Cup; "above the No.4 at FC Internazionale, there will never be another name other than Zanetti" — https://www.inter.it/en/club/hall-of-fame/javier-zanetti (same text at https://www.inter.it/en/societa_club_hall_of_fame_player/G0683/awards/Javier-Zanetti)
- Debut Aug 27, 1995 v Vicenza as a 22-year-old from Banfield; "the first of 858 appearances that would make him the player with the most matches in Nerazzurri history"; "five Serie A titles, one Champions League, one Club World Cup, one UEFA Cup, four Coppa Italia, and four Supercoppa Italiana"; "current Nerazzurri Vice President" — https://www.inter.it/en/news/30-years-on-remembering-javier-zanettis-debut-with-inter-a-nerazzurri-story
- Captain from 1999 until retirement; captained the 2010 treble; Champions League final v Bayern May 22, 2010 was his 700th Inter game; 858 appearances 1995–2014; vice-president — https://www.serpentsofmadonnina.com/2015/4/27/8503003/number-4-is-forever-thank-you-zanetti
- Last game May 18, 2014 at Verona; 143 Argentina caps; 17 managers in 19 years — https://thesefootballtimes.co/2018/12/12/javier-zanetti-il-capitano/
- Argentina: 145 caps per https://rsssf.org/miscellaneous/zanetti-intl.html (first cap Nov 16, 1994; last July 16, 2011)
