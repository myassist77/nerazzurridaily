Milan 1–2 Inter, April 22, 2024 (Serie A): Acerbi 18', Thuram 49', Tomori 80'. The 20th Scudetto and the second star, sealed with five games to spare — the first Serie A title ever decided in a Milan derby, and Inter's sixth consecutive derby win, a club record.
Inter 5–1 Milan, September 16, 2023 (Serie A): Mkhitaryan 5' and 69', Thuram 38', Çalhanoğlu 79' (penalty), Frattesi 90+3'; Leão 57' for Milan. Four scorers, a four-goal margin, the fifth derby win in a row.
Inter 6–5 Milan, November 6, 1949 (Serie A): the highest-scoring derby ever, eleven goals; Inter were 4–1 down after 19 minutes; Amadei scored three.
Milan 0–4 Inter, August 29, 2009 (Serie A): Thiago Motta 29', Milito 36' (penalty), Maicon 45+1', Stanković 67' — Mourinho's first derby of the treble season.
Inter 4–2 Milan, February 9, 2020 (Serie A): 0–2 down at half-time (Rebić, Ibrahimović), then Brozović 51', Vecino 53', de Vrij 70', Lukaku 90+3'.
Milan 0–2 Inter, May 10, 2023 (Champions League semi-final, first leg): Džeko 8', Mkhitaryan 11'; Inter won the second leg 1–0 on May 16.

## Our pick — Milan 1–2 Inter, April 22, 2024 (Serie A, matchday 33, Milan designated home)
- Scorers: Acerbi 18', Thuram 49' (4' of the second half on inter.it; 49' Sky/football-italia), Tomori 80' (81' in inter.it's head-to-head list). — https://inter.it/en/match_center/5199 ; https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11854/13121129/inter-milan-clinch-serie-a-title-with-victory-over-rivals-ac-milan ; https://football-italia.net/serie-a-milan-1-2-inter-scudetto-derby-delight/
- The 20th Scudetto, the second star, sealed with five games to spare; "the first team to mathematically clinch the Scudetto in a city derby" (inter.it analysis) and "the first time a Serie A title was sealed in a Derby di Milano" (inter.it 2026-27 derby guide); Wikipedia: "the first time the Scudetto had been decided in a Derby della Madonnina". — https://www.inter.it/en/news/analysis_scudetto_inter_record ; https://www.inter.it/en/news/history-derby-numbers-lowdown-ac-milan-inter-2026-27 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derby_della_Madonnina
- Sixth consecutive derby win — Inter's all-time record run (inter.it 2026-27 guide; Wikipedia records: 6, shared with Milan's 1911–13 and 1946–48 runs); across the six Inter scored 14 and conceded 2 (football-italia). — same URLs
- Points gap after the match: 17 (Sky Sports); final gap 19, 94 points (inter.it analysis). Kept off screen (the gap changed afterwards).
- Red cards in stoppage time: Dumfries, Theo Hernández, Calabria (Sky, football-italia). Not used on screen.
