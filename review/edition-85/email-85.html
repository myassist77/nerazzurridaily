Inter confirmed that Hakan Çalhanoğlu, the midfielder, has a muscle strain in his right thigh. Today Inter host VfB Stuttgart in the Champions League at San Siro, at 3:00 PM ET. Cristian Chivu, Inter’s head coach, said Piotr Zieliński is out too.

CONFIRMED · CLUB · LEAGUE · UEFA

Çalhanoğlu has a thigh strain

Çalhanoğlu had tests on Tuesday morning at the Humanitas clinic in Rozzano, near Milan. The club says they showed a strain in the flexor muscles at the back of his right thigh. Inter gave no recovery time.

UPDATE · INTER.IT · NOV 24

Chivu rules out Zieliński

“Piotr won’t be available tomorrow,” Chivu said on Tuesday. He added that John Stones, the center back, “is doing better and will be in the squad.”

Chivu said he has five midfielders and all three forwards available. “We can’t afford to improvise in this match,” he said.

SAID · INTER.IT · NOV 24

Stuttgart at San Siro today

Inter have six points, one behind Stuttgart. Tickets are still on sale, and gates open at 1:00 PM ET, 19:00 CET. Inter’s under-19 team plays Stuttgart in the UEFA Youth League at 9:00 AM ET, 15:00 CET, live on Inter.it.

REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Sučić to replace Çalhanoğlu

Sky Sport Italia expects Petar Sučić, the midfielder, to take Çalhanoğlu’s place in the center of midfield. It has Pio Esposito and Marcus Thuram up front, with Thuram favored over Lautaro Martínez, the captain.

SKY SPORT ITALIA · NOV 24

Tuttosport starts Mkhitaryan

Tuttosport, a Turin sports daily, has a similar lineup but starts Henrikh Mkhitaryan, the midfielder, where Sky has a different midfielder. It also lists Ange-Yoan Bonny, the forward, as unavailable.
