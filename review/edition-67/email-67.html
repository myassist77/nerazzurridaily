Inter Women go to Milan this afternoon for the first women’s derby of the season. Daniele Chiffi will referee the men’s match against Como at San Siro on Sunday, and the club says the stadium is heading toward a sellout. It is the last league match before the international break. Inter are second in Serie A with 25 points and have won all five of their home league games.

Inter Women play the first Milan derby of the season

Inter Women are away at Milan this afternoon on the sixth matchday of Serie A Women. Kickoff is 9:00 AM ET (15:00 CET) at the Puma House of Football, Milan’s stadium at the Centro Vismara.

Inter have won four of their first five league matches and drawn the other, scoring ten goals and conceding none. They host Manchester City in the Women’s Champions League on Tuesday at 3:00 PM ET (21:00 CET).

INTER.IT · AC MILAN · NOV 7

Chiffi will referee Inter against Como

Daniele Chiffi, an Italian referee from Padova, takes charge at San Siro on Sunday. Neither side has a player suspended for the match.

Inter have won their last 11 league meetings with Como. At San Siro they have 14 wins from 15 Serie A meetings, with one defeat. Marcus Thuram, Inter’s French forward, has four goals in three league matches against Como.

INTER.IT · NOV 6

San Siro is heading toward a sellout

The remaining tickets for Sunday have been released through a resale for full season-ticket holders, the club says. Italy’s national sports-events observatory has limited residents of the province of Como to the away section.

Gates open at 10:00 AM ET (16:00 CET), two hours before kickoff. Inter TV’s pre-match show starts at 10:45 AM ET (16:45 CET) on the club’s YouTube channel.

INTER.IT · NOV 7

 
REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Sportmediaset puts Carlos Augusto ahead of Dimarco

Sportmediaset has Carlos Augusto, an Inter defender, narrowly ahead of Federico Dimarco at left back against Como. That is the change from the lineup Sky Sport Italia published on Friday, which started Dimarco.

It keeps Francesco Pio Esposito alongside Marcus Thuram in attack, with Djed Spence at right back. Lautaro Martínez, Inter’s injured captain, is expected back after the international break.

UPDATE · SPORTMEDIASET · NOV 7

Tuttosport says Calhanoglu’s agent is asking for two years

Tuttosport reports that Hakan Calhanoglu, an Inter midfielder, has no option clause in his contract. From February he would be free to agree a move elsewhere. Dario Baccin, Inter’s chief football officer, has spoken with the agent Gordon Stipic, who is asking for a two-year deal worth €6.5 million (Italian salaries are quoted after tax). The paper names Como, Sunday’s opponent, among the clubs interested.
