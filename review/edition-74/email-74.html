Italy finish their UEFA Nations League group away to Belgium on Sunday night, and four Inter players travel with them. Sky Sport Italia puts the group at Italy 10 points, France 10 and Belgium 9, with avoiding defeat enough for the quarter-finals. At home, Inter Women host Juventus on Sunday with the gates open, and tickets for Inter against Liverpool on January 19, 2027 go on sale Tuesday.

Italy's group comes down to Brussels

Belgium host Italy on Sunday in the last match of the group. Kickoff is 2:45 PM ET, 20:45 CET, in Brussels.

Four Inter players are in the squad: the midfielder Nicolò Barella, the defenders Alessandro Bastoni and Federico Dimarco, and the forward Francesco Pio Esposito.

INTER.IT · NOV 11

Inter Women host Juventus with the gates open

Inter Women play Juventus on Sunday at 9:00 AM ET, 15:00 CET, in matchday 7 of Serie A Women.

The match moves to the Stadio Ernesto Breda in Sesto San Giovanni, not the usual Arena Civica. Entry is free. Rai 2 shows it in Italy, and no United States listing has been published.

INTER.IT · FIGC · NOV 14

Akanji plays 90 minutes as Switzerland go up

The defender Manuel Akanji played all 90 minutes of Switzerland’s 0–0 draw with Slovenia in Maribor on Friday. The point left Switzerland top of League B Group 1 with 13 points and promoted to League A.

Two more Inter players are in action today. The defender Carlos Augusto is with Brazil against Japan in Singapore. The midfielder Piotr Zieliński is with Poland away to Romania at 2:45 PM ET, 20:45 CET.

REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Two readings of where Çalhanoğlu's renewal stands

Sky Sport Italia’s market reporter Gianluca Di Marzio said on Tuesday that renewing the Türkiye midfielder Hakan Çalhanoğlu is Inter’s priority. His contract runs out in June, and the report has Juventus ready to sign him on a free transfer.

FCInterNews put it differently on Friday night. Inter have promised Çalhanoğlu and his agents another meeting, and he gives Inter priority. Juventus only made a few phone calls early this year, and never put an offer in writing. The same report expects Carlos Augusto to re-sign before Christmas.

SKY SPORT ITALIA · FCINTERNEWS · NOV 13

Thuram is said to be aiming at Bergamo

FCInterNews reported four injured players at Appiano Gentile, Inter’s training ground, on Saturday for solo work. They are the forwards Lautaro Martínez, Marcus Thuram and Ange-Yoan Bonny, and the midfielder Aleksandar Stanković.

Thuram picked up what Italian reports call affaticamento muscolare — a tired muscle, not a tear — in the Como match. He is expected back with the group next week and available for the Atalanta match.

Lautaro Martínez could make the bench in Bergamo. Bonny and Stanković are each said to be out about a month.
