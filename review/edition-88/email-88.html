Scans show a strain in Bastoni’s thigh

Bastoni had tests on Friday morning at the Humanitas hospital in Rozzano, near Milan. Inter said they showed a “risentimento al quadricipite della coscia sinistra” — soreness in the quadriceps of the left thigh.

What is new: the club has now confirmed the injury. Its statement gave no timeline and did not mention tonight’s match.

UPDATE · INTER.IT · NOV 27

Ayroldi referees Inter against Genoa

Giovanni Ayroldi, a referee from Molfetta in southern Italy, takes charge tonight. Inter are third with 29 points, and Genoa, coached by Daniele De Rossi, are tenth with 16.

Inter are unbeaten in their last 14 Serie A meetings with Genoa. Gates open at 12:45 PM ET, which is 18:45 CET in Milan.

INTER.IT · NOV 27

Inter mourn Juary

Inter sent condolences after the death of Juary, the Brazilian striker, born Juary Jorge dos Santos Filho in 1959. He joined Inter in 1982 and scored four goals in 36 games for the club.

After leaving Inter, he scored the winning goal against Bayern Munich in the 1987 European Cup final.

REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Who covers for Bastoni

Corriere dello Sport, the Rome sports daily, says Inter will most likely use the setup that finished against Stuttgart. Federico Dimarco, the wing back, would play on the left, and Carlos Augusto, the defender, would move into the back three.

The paper adds that Ange-Yoan Bonny, the striker, and Hakan Çalhanoğlu, the midfielder, both have a problem in the right hamstring.
