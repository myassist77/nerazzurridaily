A red mark for every player in Bergamo

Inter play Atalanta at the Gewiss Stadium today at 2:45 PM ET, 20:45 CET. Players and match officials will walk out with a red mark on their cheeks. It is part of the league’s campaign against violence against women, run with the group WeWorld.

The captains will wear special armbands, and the stadium announcer will read a message before kickoff. The International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women is Wednesday, November 25.

INTER.IT · NOV 21

Inter U23 beat Potenza 2–1

Inter’s under-23 side beat Potenza 2–1 on Saturday in Serie C, Italy’s third division, at the Stadio Breda in Sesto San Giovanni. Marello put Inter ahead in the 16th minute, Potenza’s Selleri equalized in the 23rd, and the substitute Agbonifo won it in the 85th.

The win lifts the team to fourth, with 23 points. They play again on Wednesday, away at Audace Cerignola.

INTER.IT · NOV 21

Inter’s title-and-cup double, now on film

Inter have released Solo Noi. Una stagione da Double — “Only Us. A Double Season” — a feature film about 2025-26. That season they won both the Serie A title and the Coppa Italia, Italy’s main domestic cup. The actor Stefano Accorsi narrates it.

In Italy it is on pay-per-view through Sky Primafila, DAZN and OneFootball. The club’s page also points to it on Inter’s official YouTube channel.

Jones and Pavard tipped to start

New today: Corriere dello Sport expects the midfielder Curtis Jones, now fully fit, and the defender Benjamin Pavard to start. Pavard has not started since October 10, against Parma, before his injury.

The paper says the midfielder Piotr Zieliński is out with a foot problem and the captain, Lautaro Martínez, will start on the bench. Sky Sport Italia’s predicted lineup on Saturday instead had the defender Manuel Akanji in the middle of the back three and Petar Sučić in midfield.
