# Guardrails

Written into every brief. The Lead may not relax them.

- No bought lists, fake accounts, paid followers or paid engagement. Every subscriber confirms by double opt-in.
- No automated posting into communities we do not own: Reddit, Facebook groups, forums.
- The owner's name and sign-in address never appear in an email, a post, a prompt, a file or a commit. Mail is signed "Nerazzurri Daily Support Team".
- No spending without the owner's approval, never above his cap. Approved so far: one paid test of 50 to 100 US dollars in the third week, not yet set up.
- One pitch and one follow-up per partner. No third email.
- Confirmed and reported stay separate in the edition; every item sourced. No growth test changes what the edition says.
- No match footage, club crests or commercial music. No "AI-generated" label on TikTok unless the owner says so.
- Growth agents never edit, create or delete scheduled tasks from a run, and never edit the 5:30, 6:45 or 7:30 task prompts.
