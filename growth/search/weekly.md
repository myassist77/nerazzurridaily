## Proposal: make /injuries/ the "injuries and suspensions, by player" page

This is last week's proposal, made again with three times the evidence and one addition. One change to one page, no new address:

1. Above the dated log, a "who is out" section with one entry per player who has an injury item in the last 30 days of editions: the player's name, his newest item (headline, CONFIRMED or REPORTED label, source line, date) and under it his earlier items by date with links to their editions. Each entry gets its own anchor (/injuries/#calhanoglu).
2. Under it, a short "Suspensions" section: the edition items of the last 30 days whose headline or text says a player is suspended, was sent off or is banned, newest first, with source and date, and the matchday-guide lines that say nobody is suspended.
3. The page title and description are built each morning from the same items, in the words people search: "Inter Milan injuries and suspensions: list by player, updated <date>", and a description that names the players of the newest three items with their labels.

Queries that justify it: the 15 injury searches above (43 impressions, eight at position 4 or better, 1 click in 28 days), on a page shown 121 times this week with no click. Four of them name what the page lacks: suspensions (two searches at position 1), return dates (two at position 1), a list, and "today".

Built from: the injury items the page already selects from data/edition-*.json (headline, first paragraph, source line, label, edition number, date). On Nov 26 that selection holds 35 items; the newest three are about one player (Nov 23, 24 and 25), the five before them, from Nov 10 to 14, are about three other players. Suspension items come from the same edition blocks, chosen by word (about a dozen of the 77 editions use one). A return date is printed only where an item's own text gives one, in the item's words. Nothing is written by hand and no squad list is needed.

Why it is not a thin page: it adds no address and repeats no other page. It makes the site's most shown search page answer the question its searches ask.

Risks for the Builder to settle:
- Names taken from headlines by rule can miss a player or pick a wrong word, and the editions spell one player two ways (with and without accents), so names must be compared with accents removed. If the rule is not reliable in testing, the section falls back to the dated log.
- The word rule for suspensions must not list an opponent's player as Inter's; where the item does not make the club clear, leave it out.
- Google last fetched the page on Nov 16, so a new title will take days to show in results. Judge the change two weeks after Google's first fetch of it, not on the day it goes live.

Not proposed, for the Lead to decide (neither can be built from the edition data alone):
- The missing "location" on away matches needs the home ground of each away opponent, which the editions do not hold as a field. It is a small fixed list, checked once against a source.
- A row of links to the next three match pages on the home page, /results/ and /injuries/ would give Google a path to each match page before its match. Raised last week; two more match pages have since gone unfetched. It touches shared page parts.
