Chivu says Spence and Çalhanoğlu are both available

Cristian Chivu, Inter's head coach, met reporters on Friday at Appiano Gentile, Inter's training ground. Djed Spence, the defender, has been off the field for about two months. Chivu said: “sono due mesi che è assente dai campi: ora è a disposizione e sarà convocato” — now available, and he will be in the squad.

Hakan Çalhanoğlu, the midfielder, is in the same position: “Anche Hakan ha ricominciato lunedì … ed è a disposizione del gruppo” — he restarted on Monday and is available. Chivu would not name a lineup.

SAID · INTER.IT · OCT 9

Parma at San Siro, with Bonacina refereeing

Kickoff is 12:00 PM ET, 18:00 CEST, in Serie A matchday 6. Inter are first with 13 points from five matches, four wins and a draw; Parma are 15th with 4. Kevin Bonacina referees, and the club lists no suspensions on either side.

Inter have not lost to Parma in the league in nine meetings, and Parma have won twice in 29 visits to Milan, in 1999 and 2018. The last was on May 3, when a Marcus Thuram opener set up a 2–0 win that clinched Inter's 21st Scudetto, the Italian league title. Parma arrive under a new head coach, Alberto Gilardino, appointed on September 29 after Carlos Cuesta left.

Inter Women go to Lyon on October 28, and away tickets are open

Inter Women play OL Lyonnes in Lyon on Wednesday, October 28, in matchday 3 of the Women's Champions League, kicking off at 4:00 PM ET, 21:00 CET. Seats in the away section cost 10 euros on the French club's ticketing site, and sales close on Monday, October 26 at 7:00 AM ET, 12:00 CET. They host Parma in Milan next Sunday, October 18, at 10:00 AM ET, 16:00 CEST.

Gazzetta dello Sport: a first start for Aleksandar Stankovic

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy's largest sports daily, expects Aleksandar Stankovic, the midfielder, to make his first start. It has him preferred to Hakan Çalhanoğlu in a 3-5-2, with Josep Martínez in goal and Petar Sucic alongside Nicolò Barella. Lautaro Martínez, the captain, starts rather than rests, ahead of Francesco Pio Esposito.

GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · OCT 10

Sky Sport Italia has the same shape, with Zielinski the alternative

Sky Sport Italia's Friday version gives the last midfield place to Piotr Zielinski rather than Sucic, with Henrikh Mkhitaryan the other candidate. It lists Djed Spence among the squad for the first time this season.
