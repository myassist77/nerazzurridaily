Gazzetta: talks open on Dimarco to 2030

Gazzetta dello Sport reports that Inter and Federico Dimarco have started negotiating an extension to 2030, with a meeting set between sporting director Dario Baccin and the player's agent, Arturo Canales. His current deal runs to 2027 with a club option for one more year, and the paper puts his salary at about €4 million net a season.

GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · SEP 12

Gazzetta: Stones starts, Martinez keeps the gloves

The same paper has John Stones starting in the middle of the defense against Udinese, with Manuel Akanji back from muscle fatigue. Josep Martinez stays in goal despite the error that led to Madrid's opening goal; Gazzetta writes that Chivu weighed a change to Ivan Provedel and decided against it for now.

Up front the question is Marcus Thuram, whom the paper puts at 60 to 70 percent, against Pio Esposito. Dimarco has still not rejoined full group training.
