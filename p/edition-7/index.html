Chivu on the eve: “a duty to write important pages”

Chivu spoke at the Bernabéu on Monday evening. “We have a duty to write important pages in the history of the Club,” he said, and: “We don’t want to change who we are, and we want to show courage and personality.”

On the new league phase he was precise: “In my opinion, you need 16 points. Fifteen won’t be enough, and even 16 isn’t a guarantee.” On his team sheet: “For me, it’s not important who starts, but all 16 players who will make their contribution.” Lautaro Martínez, beside him: “For us, it’s an objective. We all dream, but it’s not an obsession.”

INTER.IT · SEPT 7

Kickoff, the referee, and who cannot play

Matchday 1 kicks off at 3:00 PM ET (21:00 CEST) at the Santiago Bernabéu. Michael Oliver of England referees, with Andrew Dallas of Scotland on VAR.

Real Madrid are without Camavinga, Bernardo Silva and Güler through suspension; Inter have no suspensions. The squad trained at Appiano Gentile on Monday morning and flew out of Malpensa that afternoon.

Inter have published no lineup and will not until about an hour before kickoff.

INTER.IT · SEPT 7

Juventus in the Women’s Cup semifinal, Saturday

The draw sent Inter Women to Juventus: Saturday, September 12 at 2:30 PM ET (20:30 CEST), at the Stadio Vittorio Pozzo–Lamarmora in Biella, with Juventus at home. The final is Saturday, September 19 at 12:00 PM ET (18:00 CEST) in Viareggio.

Inter got there by coming from behind to beat Lazio 3-1 and winning Group A.

Gazzetta: five changes, and Dimarco stays behind

La Gazzetta dello Sport, carried by FCInterNews, expects about five changes from the Napoli team: Josep Martínez; Pavard, Akanji, Bastoni; Luis Henrique, Barella, Calhanoglu, Jones, Carlos Augusto; Lautaro and Thuram — “il ritorno della ThuLa,” the return of the Thuram–Lautaro pairing, with Curtis Jones in for a first start.

On Dimarco, it says the left knee he hurt against Napoli keeps him in Milan, and calls the choice a “decisione precauzionale” — a precautionary one.

The club has published nothing on the knee, and no lineup.

GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · SEPT 8

Gazzetta: Spence closing in, with Roma the target

The same paper says Djed Spence is close to training with the group again — “presto in gruppo” — after a late start to preseason, and that he is aiming at Roma away on September 19. He can play either flank, which is the shape of the hole Dimarco leaves.

Inter have said nothing about his fitness or a return date.
