Two own goals at Venezia send Inter top before the derby

Two own goals in three minutes won it for Inter at Venezia, 0–2. That put them top of Serie A with 25 points from nine, and AC Milan lost 2–4 at home to Bologna the same evening, three days before Saturday's derby. Inter Women were beaten 6–0 at OL Lyonnes.

Chivu said Inter were slow, tired and lucky

Cristian Chivu, Inter's head coach, sent on Hakan Calhanoglu, Nicolò Barella and Manuel Akanji together at half-time, with the match goalless. He told Sky Sport Italia afterward: "Siamo stati fortunati oggi nel portarla a casa" — we were lucky today to bring it home.

He credited Ivan Provedel, Inter's goalkeeper: "Provedel ci ha tenuti in partita" — Provedel kept us in the match. Chivu said the squad has to find its energy again before Saturday.

Inter Women lost 6–0 at OL Lyonnes

Marie-Antoinette Katoto scored in the 18th and 45th minutes at the OL Stadium in Décines-Charpieu. A lob from beyond the halfway line made it 3–0 in the 59th, and a substitute scored three times in the last half-hour.

It is the first defeat of the European campaign for David Sassarini's side, who won 1–0 over BK Häcken and drew 1–1 at Austria Wien. They stay on four points and play Manchester City on Tuesday, November 10.

INTER.IT · OCT 28

Milan lost 2–4 at home to Bologna

AC Milan, Inter's opponents on Saturday, lost at the Stadio Giuseppe Meazza on Wednesday evening. Samuel Mbangula scored three times for Bologna and Alphadjo Cissè twice for Milan, with an own goal in the 60th minute in between.

Inter lead Serie A with 25 points from nine, level on points and goal difference with Lazio. Milan are fifth with 18.

Inter are watching Stuttgart's Finn Jeltsch

Calciomercato.com — Italy's transfer-news site — reports that Inter's scouts have watched Finn Jeltsch several times this season. The Stuttgart defender, who can play center-back or wing-back, would cost close to €40 million.

Sport, the Barcelona daily, says Barcelona are interested too. Inter play Stuttgart at San Siro in the Champions League on November 25.

CALCIOMERCATO.COM — VIA FCINTERNEWS · OCT 28

Gazzetta says Dimarco's new contract is stuck

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy's biggest sports daily, reports that talks over a new deal for Federico Dimarco, Inter's left wing-back, have not moved since September. His contract runs to 2027. The paper says Inter hold an option to extend it by a year.

It puts the salary discussed early on at about €7.5 million (Italian salaries are quoted after tax), and says that is hard for Inter to reach. Dimarco himself said: "Purtroppo non abbiamo ancora parlato di rinnovo" — unfortunately we still haven't talked about a renewal.
