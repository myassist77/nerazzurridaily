Fiorentina at San Siro, with Lautaro Martínez still out

Inter host Fiorentina at San Siro at 7:30 AM ET, and Lautaro Martínez, the Inter captain, is still out. The club said on October 15 that tests found a strain in his left hamstring. It set no return date. Maurizio Mariani is the referee. The club counts this as the 177th league meeting: Inter have won 74, Fiorentina 46.

Inter Women are held to a 0–0 draw in Naples

Inter Women drew 0–0 away to Napoli Women on Saturday. It was their first dropped points of the Serie A Women season, after three straight wins, and they have now gone four league matches without conceding a goal.

Inter had a goal ruled out in the 52nd minute: play had already been stopped for a handball. They have 10 points from four matches. On Wednesday they travel to OL Lyonnes in the Women's Champions League at 4:00 PM ET, 21:00 CET.

Zuberek rescues a point for Inter U23

Inter U23 drew 1–1 with Sorrento at the Stadio Ernesto Breda on Saturday. Jan Zuberek, the Inter U23 forward, equalized in the 84th minute, turning in the third attempt of a goalmouth scramble. Sorrento had gone ahead in the 71st minute.

Zuberek has five goals in his last three games, and Inter U23 are eleventh in Serie C with 14 points.

INTER.IT · OCT 24

Inter extend Matias Mancuso's contract

Inter have agreed to a new deal with Matias Mancuso of the club's Under-23 side. The statement calls it a "prolungamento pluriennale" — a multi-year extension — and gives no end date.

Gazzetta expects Pio Esposito in the middle

With Lautaro Martínez out, Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy's biggest sports daily, expects a new pair up front. It has Cristian Chivu, the Inter manager, starting Francesco Pio Esposito, the Inter forward, in the center, with Marcus Thuram, the Inter forward, working around him. The paper writes "Pio al centro e Thuram a cercare spazio intorno" — Pio in the middle, Thuram looking for space around him.

It says Esposito is sure of his place after sitting out the win over Shakhtar Donetsk. Thuram is well clear of Ange-Yoan Bonny, the Inter forward, for the second spot. Inter have published no lineup.

Chivu kept the squad at the training ground overnight

Gazzetta dello Sport reports that Chivu, who has not favored pre-match camps, had the players sleep at Appiano Gentile, Inter's training ground, before this midday kickoff in Milan. They trained the previous morning, went home for the afternoon, and reconvened at 2:30 PM ET, 19:30 CET.

The team was due to leave for the stadium at about 5:30 AM ET, 10:30 CET, with a breakfast doubling as lunch. "Oggi si gioca alle 12.30, un tempo troppo corto per la solita convocazione mattutina" — today's kickoff is too early for the usual morning meet-up.
