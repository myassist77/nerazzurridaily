Chivu speaks at 8:30 AM ET, and kickoff is Tuesday at 3:00 PM ET

Inter trained at Appiano Gentile, Inter's training ground, at 5:00 AM ET on Monday. Cristian Chivu's official press conference follows there at 8:30 AM ET, which is 14:30 CEST. Club Brugge hold their own at San Siro at 12:45 PM ET, or 18:45 CEST.

Kickoff is Tuesday at 3:00 PM ET, 21:00 CEST, at San Siro. The Inter site FCInterNews had listed the press conference at 7:00 AM ET, or 13:00 CEST; the club's own time is the one printed here.

INTER.IT · OCT 11

No points yet in the league phase, and 25th of 36

Inter lost their opening league-phase match 2–1 at Real Madrid. Tuesday is the first of their four home matches in the phase.

Shakhtar Donetsk come to San Siro on Wednesday, October 21, and Inter go to Feyenoord on Tuesday, November 3.

INTER.IT · OCT 12

Club Brugge arrive second in Belgium, with Sommer in goal

Club Brugge have won seven of their first eight matches in the Belgian Pro League and sit second with 21 points, one behind Gent. In the Champions League they have no points either, after losing 3–2 at home to Aston Villa.

Ivan Leko, their head coach since 2025, plays a 4-2-3-1 that can become a 4-3-3. Inter's own analysis names the high line that Aston Villa punished on the counterattack as the way in.

Hans Vanaken, the captain, is the creative hub, and Yann Sommer, the former Inter goalkeeper, is in the Brugge goal.

INTER.IT · OCT 11

REPORTED · PRESS ONLY · NOT YET OFFICIAL

Sky Sport Italia expects five starters back for Brugge

Sky Sport Italia, relayed by FCInterNews, reports that Chivu will change the side. Lautaro Martínez, the captain and forward, is expected to start, with the midfielders Nicolò Barella and Piotr Zieliński and the defenders Alessandro Bastoni and Manuel Akanji. None of the five started against Parma.

Inter publish the lineup about an hour before kickoff.

SKY SPORT ITALIA VIA FCINTERNEWS · OCT 11

Rohit Saggi of Norway has the match, with a Spanish VAR

FCInterNews reports that Rohit Saggi, the Norwegian referee, has been appointed for Inter against Club Brugge. Carlos del Cerro Grande of Spain is on VAR.

UEFA makes the appointment, and its own page would not load this morning, so it stays under the rust bar.
