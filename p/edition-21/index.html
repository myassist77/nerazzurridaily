Milan says goodbye to Mazzola, and Inter Women debut today

Sandro Mazzola was buried on Monday at the Basilica of Sant'Ambrogio in Milan. Hundreds of supporters outside the church sang the club anthem and recited the lineup that won the 1964 European Cup. Inter Women play the first Champions League match in their history today, at home to BK Häcken of Sweden, at 12:45 PM ET.

Milan says goodbye to Sandro Mazzola

His number 8 Inter jersey lay on the coffin, which was covered in white roses. Mazzola, who died on Saturday at the age of 83, spent 17 seasons at Inter and played for no other club.

Giuseppe Marotta, Inter's president and chief executive, gave the eulogy. "È una leggenda che se ne va" — a legend is leaving us — he said, and asked the club to teach its young players who Mazzola was. Massimo Moratti, the former president and Javier Zanetti, the vice president, were among the mourners.

Outside, in Piazza Sant'Ambrogio, Inter's ultras hung a banner: "Il tuo nome resta tra le pagine della nostra storia" — your name stays in the pages of our history.

Inter Women open in Europe today against BK Häcken

Kickoff at the Stadio Brianteo in Monza is 12:45 PM ET, 18:45 CEST. It is matchday 1 of the league phase, and Inter Women's first match in the competition.

What is new since yesterday: the team trained at the Brianteo on Monday to get used to the stadium. David Sassarini, the head coach, told reporters that the aim is to learn and that he expects mistakes, his own included.

Häcken, from Gothenburg, won the Swedish title in 2025 and the first Women's Europa Cup. In the United States the only coverage is in Spanish, on ESPN Deportes.

Gazzetta: Spence will be ready for Parma

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy's biggest sports daily, reports that Djed Spence, the wing-back, will be available when Inter play Parma on October 10. He has not played a minute for Inter, having missed the first five matchdays.

What is new: the paper now puts a date on it. Spence is finishing his recovery at Appiano Gentile, Inter's training ground, and rejoins full training when the squad returns on September 28.

UPDATE · GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · SEP 22

Dimarco leaves Italy's camp with a knee problem

Federico Dimarco, the wing-back, has left Italy's training base at Coverciano and gone back to Milan. Corriere dello Sport reports a mild sprain of the left knee, picked up against Napoli on September 5.

The paper quotes the Italian federation: Dimarco has not recovered from a club injury and is unavailable. Matteo Ruggeri of Aston Villa was called up in his place. Italy face Belgium in Rome later this week.
