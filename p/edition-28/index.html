Bastoni and Esposito score as Italy win 4–1 in Bursa

Alessandro Bastoni and Francesco Pio Esposito both scored as Italy beat Turkey 4–1 in Bursa on Monday night. Pio Esposito and his brother Sebastiano became the first brothers to start a match for Italy in 111 years. At home, the players who stayed behind went back to work at Appiano Gentile, Inter's training ground.

Bastoni and Pio Esposito score in Italy's 4–1 win

Alessandro Bastoni, the Inter center back, put Italy in front in the 9th minute, turning in a corner with his left foot. Davide Frattesi, the Italy midfielder, and Michael Kayode, the Italy defender, added two more before the half hour.

Francesco Pio Esposito, the Inter forward, set up Kayode's goal. He then scored the fourth in the 50th minute, heading in after a shot from Sandro Tonali, the Italy midfielder, came back off the post. Baris Yilmaz, the Turkey forward, pulled one back right at the break.

The last brothers to start a match for Italy were Aldo and Luigi Cevenini in 1915. Bastoni played all 90 minutes, Pio Esposito 75.

INTER.IT · SEP 28

Inter are back at Appiano Gentile, and Spence trained

Inter went back to work on Monday afternoon at Appiano Gentile, Inter's training ground, with most of the squad away. Cristian Chivu, the Inter head coach, took the session.

Djed Spence, the Inter defender, and Federico Dimarco, the Inter left back, were both on the club's list of the players who trained. Dimarco did not travel to Bursa with Italy after all.

Hakan Calhanoglu, the Inter midfielder, and John Stones, the Inter center back, were not on that list.

Tuttosport: Inter are watching Anguissa's contract

Tuttosport, the Turin sports daily, says Inter are following Napoli's renewal talks with André-Frank Zambo Anguissa, the Cameroon midfielder, whose contract runs out in June 2027. There is no sign of an agreement, it says.

Inter would weigh a free transfer in 2027 if he leaves, and asked Napoli about him in May before signing Curtis Jones instead. Inter have announced nothing.

Sky Sport Italia: Inter want a quicker center back

Gianluca Di Marzio, the Sky Sport Italia transfer reporter, says Inter will look for "un difensore veloce" — a fast defender — the next time they buy. He points out that John Stones still needs time.

He calls Djed Spence, who has yet to play for Inter, a new signing in effect.
