Inter Women win at Como and go into the break on six points

Inter Women won 2–0 away at Como Women on Sunday, a second league win in two games. Karólína Lea Vilhjálmsdóttir, the Inter midfielder, scored the first from the penalty spot, given in the 59th minute for a handball on a corner.

Haley Bugeja, the Inter striker, came off the bench and made it two in the 83rd minute, running onto a pass from Benedetta Glionna. Glionna, the Inter forward, had a hand in both goals.

David Sassarini's team have six points from two games, and return on October 18 at home to Parma.

INTER.IT · OCT 4

Stankovic starts again as Serbia lose in Eindhoven

Aleksandar Stankovic, the Inter midfielder, made his second Nations League start on Sunday. Serbia lost 2–1 to the Netherlands in Eindhoven, and he came off after 66 minutes.

Serbia went behind, equalized in the first half, and then conceded the winner in the 72nd minute. They are bottom of their League A group with no points from four games.

INTER.IT · OCT 4

Nine matches in 29 days, five of them at San Siro

The club has set out the month ahead: nine matches in 29 days, six in Serie A and three in the Champions League. Five of them are at San Siro, and the first Milan derby is away on Saturday, October 31.

Quotidiano Sportivo: Calhanoglu back in full training today or tomorrow

Quotidiano Sportivo, an Italian sports daily, reports via FCInter1908 that Hakan Calhanoglu, the Inter midfielder, could be back in full training with the group today or tomorrow. He did not play in Saturday's friendly against Lumezzane.

What is new is the date. FCInterNews had him back in group training early this week; the paper now has him fit to be an option against Parma. It also expects Federico Dimarco, the Inter left back, to start on Saturday.

UPDATE · QUOTIDIANO SPORTIVO VIA FCINTER1908 · OCT 5

Corriere dello Sport: five wide players for two spots

Corriere dello Sport reports that Cristian Chivu, the Inter head coach, has all five of his wide players available for the first time this season. They are Djed Spence, Federico Dimarco, Andy Diouf, Luis Henrique and Carlos Augusto.

The paper's question is who plays on the right. Diouf has started five of Inter's six games; Spence is back after two months out and still needs match fitness. Corriere dello Sport reads Diouf as the more attacking and Spence as the more defensive.
