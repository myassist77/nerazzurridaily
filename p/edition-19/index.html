Lautaro scores twice as Inter draw 2–2 at the Olimpico

Inter captain Lautaro Martínez scored twice after halftime to rescue a 2–2 draw at Roma. Manu Koné, the Roma midfielder, had scored twice himself inside the first 37 minutes; the point leaves both clubs level at the top with 13 points each. Serie A now breaks for three weeks, and Inter do not play again until Parma visit San Siro on October 10.

Chivu keeps his halftime message in the dressing room

Cristian Chivu, Inter's head coach, was asked why his team keep falling two goals behind. He said he had already given his answer in private. “I said what I needed to say about the first half at half-time, and that stays between me and my players,” Chivu said.

He called it a good point against a very strong opponent, in a stadium where it is hard to play. He said the second half brought the right attitude, grit and personality.

A minute's silence at the Olimpico for Sandro Mazzola

Both teams gathered in the center circle before kickoff for a minute's silence in memory of Sandro Mazzola, the Inter forward whose death, aged 83, was announced on Saturday. The club called it “sixty seconds to remember an entire lifetime,” and Inter's players wore black armbands.

Giuseppe Marotta, Inter's president, told the broadcaster DAZN before the match that Mazzola wore the shirt for 17 seasons and helped make Inter great. It is now up to the club “to preserve and pass on the memory of this icon,” Marotta said.

Three weeks off before Parma

Serie A now pauses for an international break that runs three weeks. Inter's next match is Parma at San Siro on Saturday, October 10, and the Champions League follows three days later at home to Club Brugge.

Corriere dello Sport: the walls shook at halftime

Corriere dello Sport reported that Chivu's halftime talk was loud enough that the dressing-room walls “probably shook,” an account carried on by the Inter site SpazioInter. None of it is official, and Chivu would only repeat that what he said stays between him and the players.

Stones and Calhanoglu are aiming to return for Parma

Corriere dello Sport reports that John Stones, the England defender, and Hakan Calhanoglu, Inter's deep-lying midfielder, are both targeting the Parma match on October 10. The paper calls the break a blessing for the two of them.

Stones has a muscle strain in his left thigh, with no tear found. Calhanoglu has an adductor problem in his right thigh. Sky Sport Italia named the same return date last week.
