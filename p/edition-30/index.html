Lautaro Martínez matches Agüero's Argentina total

Argentina beat Bolivia 4–0 at the Estadio Mario Alberto Kempes in Córdoba. Lautaro Martínez, Inter's captain and center forward, scored in the 19th minute with a low right-footed shot from the edge of the box.

It was his 41st goal for his country. That ties him with the former Manchester City forward Sergio Agüero, third on Argentina's all-time list. Lionel Messi has 125; Gabriel Batistuta has 55.

The club filed the report on Thursday in Italy. Kickoff in Córdoba was Wednesday night.

Inter Women are in Vienna tonight, and their coach wants courage

Inter play Austria Wien at the Franz-Horr-Stadion at 12:45 PM ET, 18:45 CEST, in matchday 2 of the Women's Champions League. Inter won their opener against Häcken.

David Sassarini, Inter Women's head coach, wants "the same determination, desire and courage as always" from his players. He also asked them to be "good at playing cleanly and building our moves" in Vienna.

Cecilía Rán Rúnarsdóttir, Inter's goalkeeper, said she was "very happy and proud" to play in the Champions League. The club lists the match on Disney+ and ESPN UK, with no US broadcaster.

SAID · INTER.IT · OCT 1

A film about the Double season arrives on Monday

"Solo Noi. A Double-winning season" is a documentary from Inter Media House about the 2025/26 campaign. Inter won a 21st league title and a 10th Coppa Italia — the Italian cup.

It arrives pay-per-view on Monday, October 5, on Sky Primafila, DAZN and OneFootball. The Italian actor Stefano Accorsi narrates.

Gazzetta dello Sport: a new deal for Carlos Augusto is a priority

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy's biggest sports daily, reports that Inter will meet the representatives of Carlos Augusto soon. His contract runs to 2028. No figures were published.

The paper says Cristian Chivu, Inter's head coach, uses the Brazilian defender as a left-sided center back who can also push higher.

Gazzetta dello Sport: Inter are watching a Cagliari midfielder

Positive scouting reports on Alessandro Romano, a young midfielder at Cagliari, have reached Inter. Cagliari are obliged to buy him for 6.5 million euros and will give him a five-year contract.

The paper calls it long-term planning, with the futures of Hakan Calhanoglu and Henrikh Mkhitaryan unsettled.
