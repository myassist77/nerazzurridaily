Andrés rescues a point in Vienna with the last kick

Ivana Andrés, the Inter Women captain, scored in the 93rd minute at the Franz-Horr-Stadion to earn a 1–1 draw against Austria Wien in matchday 2 of the Women's Champions League. Modesta Uka had put the home side ahead in the 21st minute with a shot from distance.

It was Andrés's first goal in a UEFA club competition. David Sassarini, the head coach, made four changes at halftime, and his team created chances in the 72nd and 78th minutes before the ball fell to Andrés in stoppage time.

Inter have four points and sit sixth in the league phase. UEFA logs the goal in the 94th minute; the club's own report says the 93rd.

INTER.IT · OCT 1

The Supercoppa comes home, and Inter play Lazio at San Siro

Inter will play Lazio in the Supercoppa Italiana at San Siro on Tuesday, December 22, at 3:00 PM ET (21:00 CET). The 39th edition goes back to a single match after years of a four-team Final Four abroad.

Inter qualify as the Serie A champions and Lazio as the Coppa Italia finalists. Mediaset has the match in Italy.

INTER.IT · OCT 1

Bisseck on joining Inter: "it felt surreal"

Yann Bisseck, the Inter center back, spoke at the Festival dello Sport in Trento, the annual event run by Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy's biggest sports daily. "When Inter called, it felt surreal. Even after a good season, I never expected a club that big to come in for me," he said.

He named Matteo Darmian, the Inter defender, as the teammate he has learned the most from. The Italian game suits him: "All 11 players have to work together like a perfectly oiled machine," he added.

Gazzetta dello Sport: Calhanoglu trains with the group again

Hakan Calhanoglu, the Inter midfielder, took part in some of Thursday's session with the rest of the squad at Appiano Gentile, Inter's training ground. It was his first group work since his injury, and the paper expects him back for Parma.

The same report says John Stones, the Inter center back, is still working on his own and is unlikely to face Parma, with a clearer picture due early next week.

GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTER1908 · OCT 2

TeamTalk: Inter are still watching Tarik Muharemovic

Inter have kept track of Tarik Muharemovic, the 23-year-old Bosnian center back who left Sassuolo for Leeds United for €40 million in the summer. They could go back for him if Alessandro Bastoni, the Inter defender, leaves in 2027. TeamTalk, the English outlet, reported it on Thursday.
