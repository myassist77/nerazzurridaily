Lautaro plays in Messi's farewell and may miss Parma

Lautaro Martínez, Inter's captain, started at the Estadio Monumental on Tuesday night as Argentina beat Benin 3–0. Lionel Messi, in his last match for Argentina, set up Nicolás Otamendi in the 48th minute and Nico Paz in the 61st, then scored a penalty in the 71st. At Appiano Gentile, Inter's training ground, Hakan Calhanoglu was in the group again, four days before Parma.

Calhanoglu is in the group as Inter turn toward Parma

Inter trained at Appiano Gentile on Tuesday under Cristian Chivu, the head coach. Hakan Calhanoglu, the Inter midfielder, was in the squad the club listed, after an international break spent coming back from a muscle problem.

What is new is that the club itself now names him with the group. Yesterday that came from FCInterNews. Marcus Thuram, Ange-Yoan Bonny, Federico Dimarco, Benjamin Pavard, Henrikh Mkhitaryan, Curtis Jones, Djed Spence and Ivan Provedel were all named too.

John Stones, the Inter defender, is not among the names. Inter host Parma on Saturday at 12:00 PM ET, 18:00 CEST.

UPDATE · INTER.IT · OCT 6

Parma lose Almqvist before San Siro

Pontus Almqvist, the Parma forward, will not play on Saturday. The club said on Tuesday that tests found "una lesione di basso grado del polpaccio della gamba sinistra" — a low-grade tear in his left calf.

He begins rehabilitation on Wednesday. Parma gave no return date.

TyC Sport: Lautaro gets home with 48 hours to spare

TyC Sport, the Buenos Aires broadcaster, reports through FCInter1908 that Lautaro Martínez has about 11,000 kilometers — some 6,800 miles — to travel home. He lands in Milan roughly 48 hours before kickoff, which the report calls close to impossible for a start.

Inter agreed to the trip. Seven matches in 21 days follow Saturday, and TyC Sport expects Chivu to rest him at the start of that run.

TYC SPORT VIA FCINTER1908 · OCT 6

Gazzetta dello Sport: Chalobah, not Gila, was the summer plan

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy's biggest sports daily, reports through FCInter1908 that Inter's first choice for the defense last summer was Trevoh Chalobah, who went to Como instead. Mario Gila of Lazio was never pursued.

Gila said this week at the Festival dello Sport in Trento that Inter had shown interest. The paper's answer is that the defense was already full once Benjamin Pavard was back and John Stones had signed. No fee is quoted.
