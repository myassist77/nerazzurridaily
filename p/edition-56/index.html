Lautaro Martínez finishes 22nd in the Ballon d'Or

Yesterday's edition had Lautaro Martínez on the shortlist; the vote is now in. Inter said the club captain finished 22nd in the 2026 Ballon d'Or, the prize the French magazine France Football gives to the men's player of the year. It was his fifth nomination, and the club published no point total.

Kane won the award at the London Palladium, where the Brazilian Ronaldo presented it. FCInterNews put Kylian Mbappé, the France forward, second and Lamine Yamal, the Spain forward, third. Martínez was the only Serie A player among the 30 finalists.

UPDATE · INTER.IT · OCT 26

The Under-23 side play their rescheduled match today

Inter said the Under-23 team host Scafatese at the Stadio Breda in Sesto San Giovanni today at 9:30 AM ET, 14:30 CET. It is the rescheduled matchday 8 fixture in Group C of Serie C, Italy's third division.

Sky Sport Italia carries it in Italy. There is no US listing.

INTER.IT · OCT 26

Inter Women are at Lyon in the Champions League

Inter Women play OL Lyonnes, as the Lyon women's club is now called, on Wednesday at 4:00 PM ET, 21:00 CET. It is matchday 3 of the Women's Champions League league phase.

Inter beat BK Häcken 1–0 and then drew 1–1 in Vienna, where Ivana Andrés, the defender, equalized in stoppage time. David Sassarini, the Inter Women head coach, has his team sixth in the league phase with four points, level with Lyon.

They drew 0–0 at Napoli in Serie A on Saturday, October 24.

Sky Sport Italia expects Thuram to be rested at Venezia

Sky Sport Italia reported that Cristian Chivu, the Inter head coach, may rest Marcus Thuram, the France forward, at Venezia on Wednesday and rotate across the team.

The same report expects Piotr Zielinski, the midfielder who scored the winning penalty, and Petar Sucic, the midfielder, in the center. Sky Sport Italia says Alessandro Bastoni, the center back, could rest as well, with Carlos Augusto in his place.

The rest of the back line is undecided. Venezia are 20th in Serie A with three points from eight matches.

SKY SPORT ITALIA VIA FCINTERNEWS · OCT 26

Luca Pairetto has Venezia vs. Inter

FCInterNews reported the matchday 9 referee appointments made by the AIA, the Italian referees' association. Luca Pairetto takes Venezia vs. Inter at the Stadio Penzo on Wednesday, with Giacomo Camplone on the video review.

FCInterNews calls it Pairetto's second match with Inter.
