Sandro Mazzola has died, aged 83

Sandro Mazzola died in the early hours of Saturday morning. He was 83. Inter announced it themselves. The statement names president Giuseppe Marotta, vice-president Javier Zanetti and head coach Cristian Chivu among those who "mourn with profound sorrow the passing of Sandro Mazzola." No cause was given.

Seventeen seasons, 1960 to 1977: 565 official appearances, 158 goals, four league titles, two European Cups, two Intercontinental Cups. He was Serie A's top scorer in 1965 and second in the 1971 Ballon d'Or.

Flags are at half mast at the Italian federation's headquarters and at Coverciano, its technical center. A minute's silence will be held at every sporting event in Italy this weekend.

The club names Stones's injury

John Stones, the defender signed this summer, had tests on Friday at the Istituto Humanitas in Rozzano. "The tests revealed a muscle strain to the flexor muscles in his left thigh," the club wrote. "His condition will be reassessed over the coming days." No return date was set.

Edition No. 15 carried Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy's biggest sports daily, on a strain in the same thigh. New today is the club's own diagnosis.

UPDATE · INTER.IT · SEP 18

Massa takes Roma–Inter, first against first

Davide Massa referees at the Stadio Olimpico this afternoon, with Mazzoleni on VAR. Kickoff is 12:00 PM ET, 18:00 CEST.

The 187th league meeting between them, the most-played fixture in Serie A. Both arrive with 12 points: Inter have won four out of four, Roma beat Torino 2–0 last time out.

Sky: three to four weeks for Stones

Sky Sport Italia puts John Stones's recovery at three to four weeks. The target is the Parma match on October 10, the first fixture after the international break.

Edition No. 15 had him back after the break. New today is a length.

Chivu says nothing, and a call to make at left wing-back

Cristian Chivu, Inter's head coach, held no press conference on the eve of the match. Corriere dello Sport calls it the first one he has skipped this season. Gian Piero Gasperini, Roma's head coach, met reporters as usual. The paper's verdict: "due strategie diverse" — two different strategies.

Sky Sport Italia has Lautaro Martínez, Inter's forward, back in the starting eleven after resting at Udinese. FCInter1908 has the one real argument on the other flank: Federico Dimarco against Carlos Augusto at left wing-back.
