Inter Women's Champions League debut is Tuesday

Inter Women play in the Champions League for the first time on Tuesday, at home to BK Häcken of Sweden. Kickoff is 12:45 PM ET, 18:45 CEST, in Monza. Tickets start at one euro. The men are away with ten national teams, and do not play again until Parma visit San Siro on October 10.

Inter Women open in Europe against BK Häcken

Inter Women have never played in the Champions League before. They qualified by beating Wolfsburg of Germany. They open the new league phase on Tuesday at the Stadio Brianteo in Monza.

Disney+ carries every match in Europe. In the United States, ESPN's rights cover Spanish-language coverage only, on ESPN Deportes.

Inter U23 come from behind to beat Foggia 3–2

Inter's under-23 team won 3–2 at the Stadio Breda in Sesto San Giovanni on Sunday. They play in Serie C, the Italian third division. Foggia scored inside three minutes.

Matteo Milani leveled in the 5th minute. Davide Fumagalli put Inter ahead in first-half stoppage time, and Cristiano Avitabile made it 3–1 in the 63rd minute. Foggia pulled one back late. It is a second straight win, and the U23s now have 10 points.

INTER.IT · SEP 20

The club's podcast remembers Sandro Mazzola

Inter published an episode of its Internazionale Morning Club podcast on Monday about Sandro Mazzola, the forward who died on Saturday aged 83. The club titled it “Sandro, forever.”

Mazzola made 565 appearances for Inter and played for no other club. “Inter gave me something priceless: an opportunity,” he says in the episode.

Gazzetta: a week off, then back to work on September 28

Gazzetta dello Sport, Italy's biggest sports daily, reports that Cristian Chivu, Inter's head coach, has given the rest of the squad a week off. They are due back at Appiano Gentile, Inter's training ground, on Monday, September 28.

Every player left with an individual program. Three are hurt: Hakan Calhanoglu, the midfielder, John Stones, the defender, and Djed Spence, the wing-back. The paper reports optimism that all three will be fit for Parma.

UPDATE · GAZZETTA DELLO SPORT VIA FCINTERNEWS · SEP 21

Thirteen Inter players have left for national duty

FC Inter News counts 13 first-team players away with ten national teams. Italy called up the midfielder Nicolò Barella, the defenders Alessandro Bastoni and Federico Dimarco, and the forward Francesco Pio Esposito.

Lautaro Martínez, Inter's captain, is with Argentina. Their last match is on October 7, three days before Parma.
