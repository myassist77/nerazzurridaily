Inter Women open in Europe, and a ticket can cost €1

Inter Women play their first match in the league phase of the Champions League on Tuesday, against BK Häcken of Sweden, at the Stadio Brianteo in Monza. Kickoff is 12:45 PM ET, 18:45 CEST.

Seats cost €10 in the Tribuna d'Onore and €5 in the Tribuna Ovest. Season-ticket holders, Inter Club members, under-18s and women pay €1.

Stones is scanned this morning

John Stones has his scans on Thursday morning, not Wednesday. FCInter1908 reports that Inter left it a day on purpose, so the scan would read the damage clearly.

Gazzetta dello Sport describes a strain in the hamstring of his left thigh and fears about a month out. Either way he misses Saturday, and the international break covers part of it.

Edition No. 15 had Gazzetta expecting him back after the break. New today is the later scan date and a month as the fear.

Çalhanoğlu's contract is the urgent one

Hakan Çalhanoğlu's deal runs to June 30, 2027. Fabrizio Romano reports that Dario Baccin, Inter's sporting director, will sit down with the midfielder's agent Gordon Stipic shortly, and that the meeting is exploratory rather than a negotiation.

No figures are attached to it. Inter treat this as the most pressing contract on their desk.
