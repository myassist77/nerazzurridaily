Pio Esposito scores Italy's third against Türkiye

Francesco Pio Esposito came off the bench in Bologna on Monday night and scored Italy's third in a 3–1 win over Türkiye. It was his seventh goal in 13 appearances for his country. Alessandro Bastoni played the full 90 minutes and Nicolò Barella 70. At Appiano Gentile, Inter's training ground, FCInterNews has Hakan Calhanoglu back in full training, four days before Parma come to San Siro.

Pio Esposito scores Italy's third at the Dall'Ara

Francesco Pio Esposito, the Inter striker, replaced Gianluca Scamacca in the 56th minute and scored in the 88th, turning in a driven cross.

Scamacca, the Italy striker, had opened the scoring in the 26th minute. Türkiye drew level through Şimşir in the 60th. Riccardo Calafiori, the Arsenal defender, headed Italy back in front in the 64th. The crowd at the Stadio Dall'Ara was 30,040.

Alessandro Bastoni played the whole match. Nicolò Barella came off for Sandro Tonali after 70 minutes. Italy are second in their group on seven points, three behind France.

INTER.IT AND FIGC · OCT 5

Thirteen Nerazzurre leave for international duty

Inter have 13 women away with their national teams this week, from nine countries. Italy take four: Elisa Bartoli, Michela Cambiaghi, Benedetta Glionna and Elisa Polli. Iceland take two, the goalkeeper Cecilía Rúnarsdóttir and Karólína Lea Vilhjálmsdóttir.

The rest go to Belgium, Bosnia, the Dominican Republic, Haiti, Malta, Montenegro and Nigeria. Most play on October 9 and again on October 13.

INTER.IT · OCT 5

The club's film of the Double season is out

Inter Media House released Solo Noi — only us — on Monday. It is a documentary on the 2025/26 season, the club's 21st league title and 10th Coppa Italia. Stefano Accorsi, the Italian actor, narrates.

It is pay-per-view: Sky Primafila on demand, DAZN and OneFootball.

FCInterNews: Calhanoglu in with the group, Stones the only one outside it

FCInterNews reports that Hakan Calhanoglu, the Inter midfielder, trained with the group on Monday afternoon after a muscle injury. Ange-Yoan Bonny, the Inter forward, is back from duty with Ivory Coast.

What is new is Monday's session. Quotidiano Sportivo had him due back today or tomorrow; FCInterNews has him already in. The same report leaves John Stones, the Inter defender, as the only player working alone, hurt against Udinese and a doubt for Saturday.

UPDATE · FCINTERNEWS · OCT 5

Tuttosport: Anguissa, Kovacic and Bernabé on Baccin's desk

Tuttosport, via FCInter1908, reports that Dario Baccin, an Inter director, is working through midfield files for next summer. The names are André-Frank Zambo Anguissa of Napoli and Mateo Kovacic, who is out of contract at Manchester City. The third is Adrian Bernabé of Parma, whose deal runs to June 2027 and who the paper calls the low-cost option.

No fee is quoted and nothing is close. Bernabé is at San Siro on Saturday.
